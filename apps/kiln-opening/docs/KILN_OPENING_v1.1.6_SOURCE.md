# 1\. Game Overview

In Kiln Opening, each player directs a ceramic workshop inspired by one of five renowned kiln traditions associated with the Song Dynasty. Your Shifu and Apprentices gather materials, form vessels, apply glazes and decorations, compete for space in a shared kiln, develop specialised craft techniques, and fulfil Market and Imperial commissions.

Worker placement determines what your workshop prepares. **The shared firing determines what that preparation becomes.** All ceramics in the kiln share one Base Heat and one Fire card, so what you make depends partly on what everyone else decides to burn.

**Object of the game.** Score the most Victory Points (VP) from completed Orders, Imperial Progress, the Imperial Seal, kiln-tradition abilities, Craft Techniques, the end-game Exhibition and remaining Coins. The game lasts exactly five rounds.

### Core Strategic Tensions

* **Market craft:** complete flexible Market Orders to gain Coins and efficient immediate VP.
* **Imperial service:** complete demanding Imperial Orders to advance Imperial Progress, unlock Apprentices and gain VP.
* **Workshop development:** invest actions and Coins in up to two Craft Techniques that permanently change how your workshop operates — and score at the end.
* **Quality mastery:** build around Fine and Masterpiece ceramics, recover selected near-misses, and exploit high-quality commissions.
* **Kiln judgement:** claim the zone you need, then argue, bargain or mislead your way to the Base Heat that suits your glazes.

### Key Terms

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|:-:|:-:|
| Ceramic | One Vessel card as it moves through Shaped, Glazed, Fired and Delivered states. |
| Apprentice | A normal worker that receives the Apprentice effect at an action location. |
| Shifu | A player's unique master worker. It receives the Shifu effect at an action location. |
| Technique | A persistent workshop ability acquired at the Guild & Academy. |
| Preferred Heat | The heat value required by a Glaze: 1, 2, 3 or 4. |
| Base Heat | Heat 0 to 5. **Start at 2, add every revealed Contribution card's Heat adjustment, then clamp to 0–5.** |
| Global Heat | Base Heat plus the revealed Fire-card modifier. Not capped — it may fall below 0 or rise above 5. |
| Actual Heat | Global Heat plus the modifier of a ceramic's kiln space, after applicable abilities. |
| Heat Difference | The absolute difference between Actual Heat and Preferred Heat. |
| Contribution card | One of three secret kiln choices: **Bank (−1), Tend (0), Stoke (+1)**, each with its own Wood cost. |
| Order hand | Orders taken but not yet completed. |

# 3\. Setup

1. Place the Central Action Board, Shared Kiln, five-round track and Imperial Progress track in the centre of the table.
2. Set each action location to the capacity for the player count. Cover or ignore unused worker spaces.
3. **Set up the Shared Kiln.** The board is a dragon kiln: three **High (+1)** spaces sit at the firebox end, two **Middle (0)** spaces run along the tunnel, and two **Low (−1)** spaces sit at the chimney end. At **4 players** use all seven. At **3 players** cover 1 High. At **2 players** cover 1 High and 1 Middle.

   *High serves two Glazes at the common Base Heat 2 — the one that aligns there and the hotter one taking its least-bad option — so High carries roughly twice the demand of the other zones.*

4. Shuffle the 30-card Market deck and 22-card Imperial deck separately. Reveal 4 Market Orders and 4 Imperial Orders face up.
5. Separate the 15 Techniques into Forming, Glazing and Firing decks of 5. Shuffle each and reveal 2 from each discipline.
6. Shuffle the 12-card Fire deck and place it face down with room for a face-up discard pile.
7. Place Clay, Wood, Coins and Vessel cards within reach. Sort Vessel cards by Shape.
8. Each player chooses a colour and takes 1 Shifu, 5 Apprentices, 1 Imperial Progress marker and one set of three Kiln Contribution cards: **Bank the Fire, Tend the Fire and Stoke the Fire**.
9. Randomly determine the first player. In reverse turn order, each player chooses an available Kiln Player Board.
10. Each player places their Shifu and 3 Apprentices beside their board as available workers. Set the remaining 2 Apprentices aside as locked.
11. Each player receives 2 Clay, 2 Wood and 3 Coins.
12. **Opening deal.** Deal each player 2 Market Orders and 2 Imperial Orders. Each player secretly chooses **2 to keep**, in any combination, and returns the other 2. Shuffle all returned Orders back into their respective decks. Reveal kept Orders once every player has chosen.
13. Place all Imperial Progress markers on space 0 and the Round marker on Round 1.

# 4\. Round Structure

| **Phase** | **Name** | **Summary** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | Start of Round | Rotate Order displays; ready abilities; refill displays. |
| 2 | Work Phase | Players alternate placing workers and resolving actions. |
| 3 | Firing Phase | Contribution cards → Base Heat → Fire card → kiln zone → Quality. |
| 4 | Order Phase | Players complete eligible Orders in turn order. |
| 5 | Cleanup Phase | Return workers, resolve milestones, trim Order hands, pass First Player. |

### Phase 1 — Start of Round

At the start of Rounds 2, 3, 4 and 5, **discard the 2 leftmost face-up Market Order and the 2 leftmost face-up Imperial Order.** Slide the remaining cards left and refill both displays to 4. In Round 1, use the setup displays unchanged.

* Refill any incomplete Order or Technique display.
* Ready all exhausted Technique tiles.
* Reset every once-per-round Kiln Tradition ability.
* The First Player begins the Work Phase.

### Phase 2 — Work Phase

Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player places one available worker on an open action space and resolves that action immediately. Continue until every player has placed all available workers or passed.

* An Apprentice receives the Apprentice effect; the Shifu receives the Shifu effect.
* A player may use the same location more than once if spaces remain.
* A player may choose less than the maximum allowed, but unused capacity is lost.
* Passing is permanent for the current Work Phase.

### Phase 3 — Firing Phase

Resolve the procedure in §8. If the kiln is empty, skip this phase and do not reveal a Fire card.

### Phase 4 — Order Phase

In turn order, each player may complete any number of Orders. Resolve each fully before beginning another.

### Phase 5 — Cleanup Phase

1. Return every placed worker to its owner.
2. For each Imperial Progress milestone at space 1 or 3 reached or passed this round, make the corresponding locked Apprentice available. Newly unlocked Apprentices act from the next round. **If an Apprentice would unlock during Cleanup of Round 5, gain 1 VP instead for that Apprentice.**
3. Each player discards Orders of their choice down to their hand limit (3), face up to the matching discard pile.
4. Pass the First Player marker clockwise.
5. Advance the Round marker. After Cleanup of Round 5, proceed to final scoring.

# 6\. Ceramic Production

### Production Stages

1. Gain Clay and Wood at the Materials Yard.
2. Pay Clay at the Forming Studio and take the appropriate Vessel card.
3. Choose a Glaze and Decoration at the Glaze Workshop.
4. Load the glazed ceramic into an active Shared Kiln space at the Kiln Yard.
5. Resolve the Firing Phase.
6. Use the finished ceramic to complete an Order or include it in the end-game Exhibition.

### Shapes and Forming Costs

| **Shape** | **Clay Cost** | **Supply** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Bowl | 1 | 8 |
| Plate | 1 | 8 |
| Washer | 1 | 8 |
| Vase | 2 | 8 |
| Censer | 2 | 8 |

### Glazes and Preferred Heat

| **Glaze** | **Preferred Heat** | **Role** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| White | **1** | The coolest glaze. Wants a cool fire, or a Low space in a warmer one. |
| Celadon | **2** | Cool-to-middling. |
| Grey-Green | **3** | Middling-to-hot. Often paired with Crackle. |
| Moon White | **4** | The hottest glaze. Wants a fierce fire, or a High space in a middling one. |

**Choosing a Glaze is a commitment to a Base Heat**, made rounds before the vessel fires — and no single Base Heat satisfies all four. Whichever you pick, you are joining one side of an argument you have not had yet.

### Decorations

| **Decoration** | **Cost** | **Rule** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Plain | 1 Coin | No additional rule. |
| Carved | 2 Coins | Required by some Orders. |
| Impressed | 2 Coins | Required by some Orders. |
| Crackle | 2 Coins | Required by some Orders; Ge may create it during firing without paying. |

### Ceramic States

* **Shaped:** records Shape only.
* **Glazed:** records Shape, Glaze and Decoration and may be loaded.
* **Fired:** also records Quality and moves to Finished Ceramics.
* **Delivered:** placed beneath a completed Order and cannot be used again.
* **Second Firing:** may return a Standard ceramic to Glazed. It keeps Shape, Glaze and Decoration, loses Quality, and its later firing completely replaces the previous result.

# 7\. Craft Techniques

Craft Techniques are permanent workshop developments. They change the value or flexibility of existing actions but never replace the player's Kiln Tradition ability.

* Divided into Forming, Glazing and Firing disciplines, with exactly 5 tiles in each.
* Six Techniques are face up: 2 from each discipline.
* A player may own at most 2 Techniques.
* Unless a tile says otherwise, each ability may be used **once per round**. Turn the tile sideways when used and ready it at the next Start of Round.
* **Carving Knives and Seal Stamps waive Decoration costs without limit**; only their re-decoration clause is once per round.
* **Each Technique you own is worth 1 VP at the end of the game.**

### Technique Timing Categories

| **Timing** | **Examples** |
|:-:|:-:|
| During an action | Large Throwing Wheel, Measuring Calipers, Drying Frames; Carving Knives / Seal Stamps when applicable. |
| Before Contribution cards are chosen | Clay Substitution during Firing, Kiln Setting, Test Pieces. |
| After Contribution cards are revealed, before Base Heat is determined | Fuel Ledger. |
| After the Fire card is revealed | Sagger Selection. |
| After Actual Heat is calculated, before Quality is assigned | Jun, Ge. |
| After Quality is assigned | Protective Saggars, Second Firing. |
| After firing is fully resolved | Kiln Records. |
| After a normal Office main action | Connoisseur Network. |

# 8\. The Shared Kiln and Firing

### Kiln Layout

| **Players** | **High (+1)** | **Middle (0)** | **Low (−1)** | **Active Spaces** |
|:-----------:|:-------------:|:--------------:|:------------:|:-----------------:|
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 6 |
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 7 |

### The Heat Chain

Firing produces four numbers, in order. Each step produces exactly one of them.

| **Step** | **What happens** | **Produces** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **1. Contribution** | Every player with at least one ceramic in the kiln secretly chooses one Contribution card they can afford. Reveal together and pay its Wood cost. Resolve any post-reveal Contribution effects such as Fuel Ledger, then add the final Heat adjustments to the starting value of 2 and clamp to 0–5. | **Base Heat** — 0 to 5 |
| **2. Fire** | Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier (−2 to +2). | **Global Heat** |
| **3. Kiln zone** | Each ceramic adds the modifier of its own space (+1 / 0 / −1). | **Actual Heat** |
| **4. Compare** | Measure each Actual Heat against its glaze's Preferred Heat. | **Quality** |

**Base Heat and Global Heat belong to the whole kiln. Actual Heat belongs to one ceramic.** Everyone chooses how hard to pull the shared fire, then every pot experiences that fire from a different place in the kiln.

| **Term** | **Meaning** |
|:-:|:-:|
| **Base Heat** | 0 to 5. Start at **2**, add every revealed Contribution card's Heat adjustment, then clamp to 0–5. Shared. |
| **Global Heat** | Base Heat + the Fire card. Shared. Not capped — it may fall below 0 or rise above 5. |
| **Actual Heat** | Global Heat + that ceramic's kiln-space modifier. Individual to each ceramic. |
| **Preferred Heat** | What the glaze wants: White 1 · Celadon 2 · Grey-Green 3 · Moon White 4. |
| **Heat Difference** | The absolute gap between Actual Heat and Preferred Heat. This becomes Quality. |

### Contribution Cards

Every player has the same three cards. A card's **Heat adjustment** affects Base Heat; its **Wood cost** is what that player pays when cards are revealed.

| **Contribution card** | **Wood cost** | **Heat adjustment** | **Meaning** |
|:-|:-:|:-:|:-|
| **Bank the Fire** | 1 | **−1** | Deliberately restrain and bank the firing. |
| **Tend the Fire** | 0 | **0** | Maintain the neutral fire without pushing it. |
| **Stoke the Fire** | 1 | **+1** | Feed the kiln enough to raise the fire one step. |

A player must be able to pay the chosen card's printed Wood cost when cards are revealed. **Tend the Fire is always affordable.** Tend represents the kiln's stable firing; Bank and Stoke are the normal one-step contest.

### Determining Base Heat

**Base Heat = 2 + the sum of all revealed Heat adjustments, clamped to 0–5.**

Examples:

* Tend + Tend + Tend → Base Heat **2**.
* Stoke + Tend + Tend → Base Heat **3**.
* Bank + Tend + Tend → Base Heat **1**.
* Stoke + Bank + Tend → Base Heat **2** — the opposing players each spent 1 Wood and cancelled each other.
* Two net Banks are enough to reach Base Heat **0**. Base Heat **4** normally requires two net Stokes, and Base Heat **5** normally requires three net Stokes; Fuel Ledger can supply one additional +1 after contributions are revealed.

The neutral fire is Base Heat 2. Moving it one step cooler or hotter normally costs somebody 1 Wood. **Cooling is no longer free, and heating no longer requires a rebate calculation.**

### What your loading tells the table

Zones are chosen in the Work Phase and are **public**. Because each Glaze aligns in a different zone at each Base Heat, every loaded ceramic announces the Base Heat its owner would most like:

| **Your placement** | **You want Base Heat** |
|:-:|:-:|
| White in Low · Celadon in Middle · Grey-Green in High | **2** |
| Celadon in Low · Grey-Green in Middle · Moon White in High | **3** |
| White in Middle · Celadon in High | **1** |
| Grey-Green in Low · Moon White in Middle | **4** |

By the time Contribution cards are chosen, everyone can read everyone else's likely target. The secret choice is therefore a tug-of-war with visible sides: who will Tend, who will pay to Bank, and who will pay to Stoke harder?

### Which Glaze each zone serves

| **Base Heat** | **Low (−1)** | **Middle (0)** | **High (+1)** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **0** | — | — | **White** |
| **1** | — | **White** | **Celadon** |
| **2** | **White** | **Celadon** | **Grey-Green** |
| **3** | **Celadon** | **Grey-Green** | **Moon White** |
| **4** | **Grey-Green** | **Moon White** | — |
| **5** | **Moon White** | — | — |

**No Base Heat serves all four Glazes.** Someone is always unserved and therefore has a reason to argue for a different fire. Base Heat 0 and 5 are extreme states that serve only one Glaze exactly.

### Quality Table

| **Heat Difference** | **Quality** | **Order Eligibility** |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 0 | Masterpiece | Satisfies any minimum Quality. |
| 1 | Fine | Satisfies Fine+ and Standard+. |
| 2 | Standard | Satisfies Standard+ only. |
| 3 or more | Flawed | Cannot satisfy Orders or the end-game Exhibition. |

### Worked Firing Example

*Three players have ceramics in the kiln.*

Ru has a **Celadon** plate in **Middle**, which is exact at Base Heat 2. Ge has a **Moon White** censer in **High**, which is exact at Base Heat 3. Ding has a **White** washer in **Low**, which is exact at Base Heat 2. Their loading has already made the disagreement visible.

**Contribution.** Ru chooses **Tend the Fire** (0 Wood, 0 Heat). Ge chooses **Stoke the Fire** (1 Wood, +1 Heat). Ding chooses **Bank the Fire** (1 Wood, −1 Heat). Reveal together and pay the Wood. The +1 and −1 cancel, so Base Heat remains **2**.

**Fire.** Reveal **+1**. Global Heat becomes **3**.

**Kiln zones and Quality.**

* Ru's Celadon plate in Middle has Actual Heat **3** against Preferred Heat 2 → difference 1 → **Fine**.
* Ge's Moon White censer in High has Actual Heat **4** against Preferred Heat 4 → difference 0 → **Masterpiece**.
* Ding's White washer in Low has Actual Heat **2** against Preferred Heat 1 → difference 1 → **Fine**.

Ge's Stoke and Ding's Bank cost the same and cancelled each other at the Base Heat step, but the shared Fire card then shifted the whole kiln in Ge's favour. That is the central firing tension: players control the **direction** of the kiln together, while the Fire deck still decides how cleanly that plan lands.

**Fuel Ledger example.** If Ge owned Fuel Ledger, after seeing the revealed Bank / Tend / Stoke cards Ge could spend **1 additional Wood**. Ge's Stoke would become +2 instead of +1, making the net Contribution adjustment +1 and Base Heat **3** before the Fire card is revealed.

**Abilities.** Several Traditions and Techniques interrupt this chain at fixed moments. The complete sequence is in **Appendix D**.

# 9\. Orders and Imperial Progress

### Taking Orders

Orders are taken at the Market & Imperial Office. For each Order taken, choose either a face-up Order from either display or a blind top-card draw from either deck. Reveal a blind-drawn card immediately.

### Completing Orders

1. Choose one Order in your hand during your turn in the Order Phase.
2. Select the required finished, undelivered ceramics.
3. Confirm every Shape, Glaze, Decoration, relationship and minimum Quality requirement. "Any" means no restriction.
4. Place used ceramics beneath the completed Order; they cannot be used again.
5. Record printed VP immediately, gain printed Coins, and place the Order face up in your scoring area.
6. **If Imperial: advance Imperial Progress by the amount printed on that Order.** Progress cannot exceed 5.

**Progress is printed per card.** Every Imperial Order grants **1 Progress per ceramic required**: single-ceramic Orders give +1, two-ceramic Orders give +2, and three-ceramic Orders give +3.

### Imperial Progress Track

| **Space** | **Title**                  | **Reward When Reached**            | **End-game VP** |
|:---------:|:--------------------------:|:----------------------------------:|:---------------:|
| 0         | Local Workshop             | None                               | 0               |
| 1         | Local Renown               | Unlock 1 Apprentice during Cleanup | 0               |
| 2         | Prefectural Recommendation | None                               | 2               |
| 3         | Court Examination          | Unlock 1 Apprentice during Cleanup | 2               |
| 4         | Awaiting Audience          | None                               | 4               |
| 5         | Imperial Audience          | Take Imperial Seal if available    | 8               |

* **Court Patronage** provides +1 Progress for 4 Coins as a Shifu Office action, **only after you have completed at least 1 Imperial Order**. It cannot reach space 5.
* When an advance reaches or passes space 1 or 3, mark the Apprentice milestone. If this occurs during Cleanup of Round 5, gain 1 VP instead.
* The first player to reach space 5 takes the Imperial Seal, worth 2 VP. Reaching space 5 does not end the game.

### End-game Exhibition

At game end, every player may exhibit finished, undelivered ceramics of Standard Quality or better.

| **Presented Quality** | **VP** |
|:---------------------:|:------:|
| Standard              | **2**  |
| Fine                  | **3**  |
| Masterpiece           | 5      |

* Choose 3 exhibited ceramics as your featured collection. Gain +2 VP if they have 3 different Shapes and +2 VP if they have 3 different Glazes.
* Flawed ceramics cannot be exhibited.

# 10\. Kiln Tradition Abilities

Unless stated otherwise, each ability may be used once per round and resets at the Start of Round.
### Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection

Once per round, **when you complete an Order using a Masterpiece with Celadon glaze and Plain decoration**, gain **4 VP**.

### Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage

Once per round, when you complete an Imperial Order, **gain 2 Coins and 1 VP**. You may also ignore one Decoration requirement on that Order.

### Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle from Fire

Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated but before Quality is assigned, spend 1 wood, choose one of your ceramics whose Actual Heat differs from its Preferred Heat 1 or 2. Set its Actual Heat equal to its Preferred Heat and change its Decoration to Crackle without paying the Crackle cost.

### Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production

Once per round, when resolving the Forming Studio, after you shape a Bowl, Plate or Washer, you may shape one additional vessel of the same Shape at normal Clay cost. This does not count against the action's normal limit.

### Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation

Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated but before Quality is assigned, you may pay 2 Wood to adjust the Actual Heat of one of your ceramics by +1 or −1.

### Ability Timing

* If several players use abilities in the same window, resolve in turn order beginning with the First Player.
* **Ge and Jun act in the same window and both change Actual Heat.** Resolve in turn order and recalculate heat difference after each.
* **Ru resolves during the Order Phase**, at the moment the qualifying ceramic is delivered — not during firing. A Masterpiece that is exhibited or left unused never triggers it.
* Guan and Ding resolve during the Order or action that triggers them.

# 11\. End of the Game and Scoring

The game ends after completing Cleanup in Round 5.

1. Begin with all VP already recorded during play.
2. Add the end-game VP shown on your final Imperial Progress space.
3. Add 2 VP if you hold the Imperial Seal.
4. **Add 1 VP for each Craft Technique you own.**
5. Resolve and add your end-game Exhibition.
6. Score 1 VP for every 3 Coins remaining, to a maximum of 5 VP.

The player with the most VP wins.

### Tie Breakers

1. The tied player farther along Imperial Progress.
2. The tied player with more completed Imperial Orders.
3. The tied player with more Masterpiece ceramics delivered or exhibited.
4. If still tied, the tied players share victory.

# 12\. General Clarifications

* Resources are limited by the supply. If a supply is empty, receive only what remains.
* Players may not trade resources, Orders, Techniques or ceramics. The Materials Yard Shifu exchange is with the general supply.
* A worker action resolves immediately. Unused action capacity is lost.
* A newly unlocked Apprentice cannot act until the next round.
* Shaped, Glazed and Finished ceramics remain in your workshop between rounds and keep all recorded attributes.
* Only delivered, exhibited or sold ceramics leave the Finished Ceramics area, except a Standard ceramic returned to Glazed by Second Firing.
* A ceramic sold by the Office or Connoisseur Network returns its Vessel card to the matching Shape supply.
* A player may not voluntarily discard a non-Flawed ceramic.
* Uncompleted Orders and unused ceramics have no end-game penalty.
* If an Order deck is empty, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
* If the Fire deck is empty, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
* If a Technique deck is empty, do not refill its display unless a tile is returned.
* Covered kiln spaces are not empty spaces; no loading or movement effect may use them.
* Covered spaces follow Setup: at 3 players cover 1 High; at 2 players cover 1 High and 1 Middle. No covered space is ever considered empty.

# Appendix D — Full Firing Sequence

*The complete order of operations, including every ability window. Cards never cite these step numbers; use the timing language printed on the cards and tiles.*

1. **Pre-firing abilities.** In turn order from the First Player, resolve optional abilities that occur before Contribution cards are chosen, such as Clay Substitution, Kiln Setting and Test Pieces.
2. **Choose Contributions.** Every player with at least one ceramic in the kiln secretly chooses Bank the Fire, Tend the Fire or Stoke the Fire. You must be able to pay the card's printed Wood cost.
3. **Reveal and Pay.** Reveal all selected cards simultaneously and pay their printed Wood costs.
4. **Post-reveal contribution abilities.** Resolve Fuel Ledger and any similar effects that occur after Contribution cards are revealed but before Base Heat is determined. Any additional Wood is paid now, and modified Heat adjustments are applied before calculating Base Heat.
5. **Determine Base Heat.** Start at 2, add every final Contribution-card Heat adjustment, then clamp the result to 0–5.
6. **Repositioning.** In turn order, each player who placed their Shifu at the Kiln Yard this round may move one of their loaded ceramics to a different empty active kiln space.
7. **Reveal Fire.** Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier (−2 to +2) to Base Heat. This is Global Heat. Do not cap the value.
8. **After-Fire abilities.** Resolve Sagger Selection and similar effects.
9. **Calculate Actual Heat.** For each ceramic, add its kiln-space modifier to the applicable Global Heat.
10. **Heat adjustments.** In turn order, resolve abilities that change Actual Heat: **Jun** and **Ge**.
11. **Assign Quality.** Compare each final Actual Heat to its Preferred Heat and use the Quality table.
12. **After-Quality abilities.** In turn order, resolve Protective Saggars and Second Firing.
13. **After-firing effects.** Resolve Kiln Records. Return remaining ceramics to Finished Ceramics, empty the kiln and discard the Fire card face up.
