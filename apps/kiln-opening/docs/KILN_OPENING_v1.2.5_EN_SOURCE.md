# KILN OPENING 开窑 v1.2.5 — Player Rulebook

**2–4 players | 90–120 minutes | Ages 12+**
*Build the workshop. Share the kiln. Master the craft.*
A medium-weight worker-placement game about Song Dynasty ceramic workshops.
# Game Overview
In **Kiln Opening**, each player directs a ceramic workshop inspired by one of five renowned kiln traditions associated with the Song Dynasty. Your Shifu and Apprentices gather materials, form vessels, apply glazes and decorations, compete for Orders and specialised knowledge, and load ceramics into a shared kiln whose final firing is influenced by every participating workshop.
**Worker placement determines what your workshop prepares. The shared firing determines what that preparation becomes.** All ceramics in the firing share one Base Heat and one Fire card, so what you make depends partly on what everyone else decides to burn.
The game lasts exactly **5 rounds**.

# Setup
1. Place the Central Action Board and Shared Kiln in the centre. For Shared Kiln:
   - **4 players:** use all 7 spaces.
   - **3 players:** use 6 spaces; do not use 1 High space.
   - **2 players:** use 5 spaces; do not use 1 High and 1 Middle space.
2. Shuffle the **48-card Main Order deck** and reveal **5 face-up Main Orders** from left to right.
3. Shuffle the separate **16-card Starting Order deck**. Deal **4 Starting Orders to each player**. Each player secretly keeps **2**. After everyone has chosen, reveal all kept Starting Orders and return all unkept and undealt Starting Orders to the box. Starting Orders are never shuffled into the Main Order deck.
4. Separate the 15 Advanced Techs into Forming, Glazing & Decoration and Firing decks of 5. Shuffle each discipline and reveal **2 face-up tiles from each**.
5. Shuffle the 12-card Fire deck and place it face down beside a face-up discard pile.
6. Place Clay, Wood, Coins and Vessel cards within reach. Sort Vessel cards by Shape.
7. Place the **4 Imperial Kiln tiles** beside the Imperial Recognition track.
8. Each player chooses a player colour and takes:
   - 1 Shifu;
   - 3 Apprentices;
   - 1 Imperial Recognition marker;
   - 1 Bank the Fire, 1 Tend the Fire and 1 Stoke the Fire Contribution card.
   Place that colour's **Imperial Priority token** on Recognition space **3**.
9. Randomly choose the First Player. In reverse turn order, each player chooses an available Kiln Player Board.
10. Each player places **1 Shifu + 3 Apprentices** as available workers.
11. Leave each player’s Imperial Kiln area empty until it is gained through Imperial Recognition.
12. Each player receives **2 Clay, 2 Wood and 3 Coins**.
13. Each player chooses **1 Starting Tech** and places it on their player board. Starting Techs are public information.
14. Place all Imperial Recognition markers on space **0** and the Round marker on Round **1**.
# Round Structure
| **Phase** | **Name** | **Summary** |
|:-:|:-|:-|
| 1 | Start of Round | Rotate Orders; refill displays; ready abilities. |
| 2 | Work Phase | Alternate worker placements. |
| 3 | Firing Phase | Contributions → Base Heat → Shifu adjustment → Fire → Actual Heat → Quality. |
| 4 | Order Phase | Complete public or held Orders one at a time. |
| 5 | Cleanup | Return workers; trim Order hands; pass First Player. |
## Phase 1 — Start of Round
In Rounds 2–5:
1. Discard the **3 leftmost face-up Main Orders** to the Main Order discard pile.
2. Slide the remaining **2 Main Orders** left.
3. Refill the display to **5**.
Then:
- ready all abilities marked **once per round**;
- the First Player begins the Work Phase.
## Phase 2 — Work Phase
Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player places one available worker at an action location and resolves it immediately. Continue until all players have passed.
- Multiple spaces are available for each action. The worker a player placed may go on any unoccupied space, even if there are other workers(the same player's or opponents) on other spaces on the same action. 
- A **Shifu** follows Apprentice placement rules except that it may be placed on a fully occupied action location. Multiple Shifu may overfill the same location.
- You may place a worker only if you can perform at least **1 instance of that location’s main action**. “Up to 2” means **1 or 2, never 0**. Optional bonuses may still be declined.
- Passing is permanent for the Work Phase.

|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Apprentice | Apply a Glaze and Decoration to **1 shaped vessel**; pay the Decoration cost. |

### Kiln Yard
| **Worker** | **Effect**                                                   |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Apprentice | Load 1.                                                      |
| **Shifu**  | Load up to 2 ceramics. After Contributions are revealed and Base Heat is determined, but before the Fire card is revealed, you may move 1 of your ceramics in the Shared Kiln to an empty active space in a neighbouring heat zone (High ↔ Middle ↔ Low). |
- Covered Shared Kiln spaces are never empty or usable. The Imperial Kiln can hold only 1 ceramic per firing.
- If multiple players used a Kiln Yard Shifu this round, resolve these repositions in **First Player order**.
- The Shifu reposition cannot move a ceramic into or out of an Imperial Kiln.

### Commission Market
For each reservation, choose one:
- reserve **1 face-up Main Order**, then refill that display position immediately; or
- reserve the **top card of the Main Order deck without looking at it first**. Reveal it after taking it.
| **Worker** | **Effect** |
|:-|:-|
| Apprentice | Reserve **1 Main Order**. Then gain **1 Clay, 1 Wood or 1 Coin**. |
| **Shifu** | Reserve **up to 2 Main Orders**, resolving each reservation separately. **After each Order you reserve, gain 1 Clay, 1 Wood or 1 Coin.** |
- A Shifu must reserve at least 1 Order, but may stop after the first reservation.
- There is no Order hand limit during a round. During Cleanup, discard Orders of your choice until you hold no more than **3**. Starting Orders and reserved Main Orders both count toward this limit. Held Orders are public information.
### Guild & Academy
| **Worker** | **Effect**                                                   |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| Apprentice | Pay the printed Coin cost and take **1 face-up Advanced Tech**. |
| **Shifu**  | Choose 1 discipline and look at the top 2 Techs of that deck, **or as many as remain**. Then take either 1 face-up Advanced Tech from any discipline or 1 of the revealed Techs, paying 1 Coin less, minimum 0. Put any revealed Tech not taken on the bottom of its deck. |
- After a tile is taken, refill its discipline to 2 if possible.
- A player may own at most **2 Advanced Techs**.
- An Apprentice placement is legal only if you can afford at least 1 face-up Tech. A Shifu placement is legal only if you can afford at least 1 face-up Tech after the Shifu discount; this guarantees a legal fallback if the revealed Techs are unaffordable.
- A newly acquired Advanced Tech may be used later in the same round if its timing permits.
### Labour
| **Worker** | **Effect** |
|:-|:-|
| Apprentice | Gain **2 Coins**. |
| **Shifu** | Gain **4 Coins**. |

## Phase 3 — Firing Phase
If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, **skip the Firing Phase and do not reveal a Fire card**.

| **Step** | **What happens** | **Produces** |
|:--:|--|--|
| 1. Contributions | Participating players secretly choose Bank, Tend or Stoke and any Fuel Ledger commitment, then reveal and pay Wood. Start at 2 and clamp the final result to 0–5. | **Base Heat** |
| 2. Shifu adjustment | In First Player order, eligible Kiln Yard Shifu may reposition 1 of their Shared-Kiln ceramics to an empty active space in a neighbouring heat zone. | — |
| 3. Fire | Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier. | **Global Heat** |
| 4. Position | Shared-Kiln ceramics add High +1 / Middle 0 / Low −1. Imperial Kiln ceramics use no zone modifier. | **Actual Heat** |
| 5. Compare | Compare Actual Heat with Preferred Heat after applicable abilities. | **Quality** |

- **Base Heat and Global Heat belong to the whole firing. Actual Heat belongs to one ceramic.**
- Clamp Base Heat to 0–5. Global Heat and Actual Heat are not clamped.

## Contribution Cards

Every player has the same 3 cards:

| **Contribution** | **Wood** | **Heat** |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Bank the Fire | 1 | −1 |
| Tend the Fire | 0 | 0 |
| Stoke the Fire | 1 | +1 |

A player participates in Contributions if they have at least 1 ceramic in either the **Shared Kiln or their Imperial Kiln**.

Tend is always affordable. After the firing, return all Contribution cards to their owners.

### Base Heat

**Base Heat = 2 + sum of final Contribution modifiers, clamped to 0–5.**

Examples:
- Tend + Tend + Tend → 2.
- Stoke + Tend + Tend → 3.
- Bank + Tend + Tend → 1.
- Stoke + Bank + Tend → 2; both players still pay their Wood.
## Quality Table

| **Heat Difference** | **Quality** | **Order Eligibility** |
|:-:|:-|:-|
| 0 | **Masterpiece** | Meets any minimum Quality. |
| 1 | **Fine** | Meets Fine+ and Standard+. |
| 2 | **Standard** | Meets Standard+ only. |
| 3+ | **Flawed** | Cannot complete Orders or enter the Exhibition. |

After all after-Quality abilities for the firing have resolved, each player may discard **1 ceramic that is still Flawed from this firing** to gain **2 Coins**. Return its Vessel card to the matching Shape supply.
## Imperial Kiln
When you first reach **Imperial Recognition 2**, take an Imperial Kiln tile and place it in your Imperial Kiln area.

- The Imperial Kiln can hold **1 ceramic per firing**.
- During a Kiln Yard action, one ceramic loaded by that action may be placed in your empty Imperial Kiln instead of the Shared Kiln.
- A Tech effect may load the Imperial Kiln only if it explicitly says so; **Rapid Drying** does.
- Its ceramic fires during the normal shared Firing Phase.
- Its ceramic has **Actual Heat = Global Heat** before other abilities and receives no High/Middle/Low modifier.
- It counts for Contribution participation and firing abilities.
- It does not occupy a Shared Kiln space and cannot be moved by the Kiln Yard Shifu.
- After firing, its ceramic resolves Quality and moves to the owner's Finished Ceramics area normally.
At **Imperial Recognition 3**, take your coloured Imperial Priority token from the track. **Once per game, before or after your worker action, spend this token to load 1 Glazed ceramic into your empty Imperial Kiln.** The ceramic must be unloaded before you load it; Imperial Priority cannot move a ceramic from the Shared Kiln or another kiln. The Imperial Kiln still holds only 1 ceramic.

## Phase 4 — Order Phase
The Order Phase uses **reverse Work-Phase seating order**: the player immediately counter-clockwise from the First Player acts first, then play proceeds counter-clockwise, with the First Player acting last in each circuit.
On each Order opportunity, a player may complete **at most 1 Order** or pass.
A completed Order may be:
- one of the player's held Orders; or
- one of the **5 face-up Main Orders**.
If a face-up Main Order is completed, refill its display position immediately.
Continue cycling in reverse turn order until one full circuit occurs in which **no player completes an Order**. Passing on one opportunity does not prevent that player from completing an Order on a later circuit if the display changes.
## Phase 5 — Cleanup
1. Return all workers to their owners.
2. Discard held Orders of your choice down to the hand limit of 3. Return discarded Starting Orders to the box; place discarded Main Orders in the Main Order discard pile.
3. If this was Round 5, proceed to final scoring. Otherwise, pass the First Player marker clockwise and advance the Round marker.


# Ceramic Production
## Production Stages
1. Gain Clay and Wood.
2. Form a vessel.
3. Apply a Glaze and Decoration.
4. Load the glazed ceramic into the Shared Kiln or, if available, an Imperial Kiln.
5. Resolve the common Firing Phase.
6. Use the finished ceramic to complete an Order or keep it for the end-game Exhibition.
Starting and Advanced Tech may compress these stages.
## Shapes and Forming Costs
| **Shape** | **Clay Cost** | **Cards Included** |
|:-|:-:|:-:|
| Bowl | 1 | 10 |
| Plate | 1 | 10 |
| Brush Washer | 1 | 10 |
| Vase | 2 | 10 |
| Censer | 2 | 10 |
Vessel cards are **not a hard gameplay limit**. If all 10 cards of a Shape are already in use, use a suitable proxy for that Shape until a matching Vessel card becomes available.
## Glazes and Preferred Heat
| **Glaze** | **Preferred Heat** | **Role** |
|:-|:-:|:-|
| White | **1** | Coolest glaze. |
| Celadon | **2** | Cool-to-middling. |
| Grey-Green | **3** | Middling-to-hot; often compatible with Crackle. |
| Moon White | **4** | Hottest glaze; normally demands a hotter firing. |
Choosing a Glaze commits the ceramic to a preferred firing outcome before the shared fire is known.
## Decorations
| **Decoration** | **Cost** |
|:-|:-:|
| Plain | 1 Coin |
| Carved | 2 Coins |
| Impressed | 2 Coins |
| Crackle | 2 Coins |
## Ceramic States
- **Shaped:** records Shape only.
- **Glazed:** records Shape, Glaze and Decoration and may be loaded. A Glazed ceramic is **unloaded** while it remains in your workshop and is not in the Shared Kiln or Imperial Kiln.
- **Fired:** also records Quality and moves to Finished Ceramics.
- **Delivered:** committed to a completed Order and cannot be reused.
# Workshop Development
A player's long-term workshop engine consists of:
> **1 Starting Tech + 1 Kiln Tradition + up to 2 Advanced Techs**
## Tech Effects and Worker Actions
**A Tech effect is not a worker action.** If a Starting Tech or Advanced Tech lets you perform part of a worker action—such as forming, glazing, decorating or loading—that does **not** count as taking that worker action and does not trigger effects that require that action, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
## Starting Tech
Each player chooses exactly **1 Starting Tech** during Setup. It does not count toward the Advanced Tech limit and scores no VP by itself. See **Appendix B** for all Starting Tech abilities.
## Advanced Tech Rules
- Exactly 5 Forming, 5 Glazing & Decoration and 5 Firing Advanced Techs exist.
- A player may own at most 2.
- Advanced Techs modify existing actions, production or firing; **no Advanced Tech creates a worker position**.
- Follow each tile's printed timing and usage limit.
- If you control multiple abilities with the same timing, choose the order in which you resolve them. If several players have abilities in the same timing window, resolve players in First Player order; each player chooses the order of their own abilities.
- Each Advanced Tech you own scores **1 VP at game end**.
See Appendix B for all 15 tiles.
# Orders and Imperial Recognition
## Main Order Market
There is one **48-card Main Order deck** and one **5-card public display**. The separate 16-card Starting Order deck is used only during Setup.
Orders may award **VP, Coins and 0–3 Crowns**.
Commercial Orders tend to be more flexible and pay more Coins. Crown Orders tend to be more specific, pay fewer Coins and advance Imperial Recognition.
## Reserving Orders
Use the Commission Market during the Work Phase to reserve Main Orders. Each reservation may take either a face-up Order or the top card of the Main Order deck without looking first, unless an ability such as **Colour Samples** changes that reservation.

A reserved Main Order:
- is placed in your hand and revealed to all players;
- is protected from other players;
- counts toward your hand limit during Cleanup;
- can be completed during any later Order Phase;
- scores exactly as printed.
## Completing Orders
During an Order opportunity:
1. Choose either 1 held Order or 1 face-up Main Order.
2. Select the required finished, undelivered ceramics.
3. Confirm all Shape, Glaze, Decoration, relationship and minimum Quality requirements.
4. Place the used ceramics beneath the completed Order.
5. Record the printed VP.
6. Gain the printed Coins.
7. Resolve **1 Crown at a time**. Each Crown advances Imperial Recognition by 1 until you reach Recognition 4. After you are at Recognition 4, each further Crown scores **1 VP immediately**. Resolve every milestone crossed.
8. Place the completed Order face up in your scoring area.
9. If it came from the public display, refill the empty position immediately.
A ceramic may satisfy only one completed Order.
### Reading Multi-Ceramic Requirements
Requirements are **independent unless a specific pairing is explicitly printed**.
For example, if an Order says:
> **Bowl + Brush Washer; one White and one Moon White; one Plain and one Impressed**
you must use exactly one Bowl and one Brush Washer, exactly one White and one Moon White Glaze, and exactly one Plain and one Impressed Decoration. You may assign the two Glazes and the two Decorations between the two Shapes independently.
If a card intends a fixed pairing, it writes the pairing directly, for example **“Bowl: White + Plain; Brush Washer: Moon White + Impressed.”**
## Imperial Recognition Track
Only **Crown icons on completed Orders** advance Imperial Recognition.
| **Recognition** | **Milestone**     | **Reward when first reached**                                |
|:---------------:|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0               | Local Workshop    | —                                                            |
| 👑 **1**         | Imperial Grant    | Gain **3 Coins** or **1 Clay + 1 Wood + 1 Coin**.            |
| 👑👑 **2**        | Imperial Gift     | Gain your **Imperial Kiln tile**.                            |
| 👑👑👑 **3**       | Imperial Priority | Take your coloured **Imperial Priority token**. Once per game, before or after your worker action, spend it to load **1 Glazed ceramic** into your empty Imperial Kiln. |
| 👑👑👑👑 **4**      | Imperial Audience | Gain **6 VP**.                                               |
| +👑              |                   | +1 VP                                                        |
- After you are at Recognition 4, **each additional Crown you gain scores 1 VP immediately**.
- Resolve Crowns **one at a time**. A multi-Crown Order may cross several milestones, reach Recognition 4 and then score VP from any remaining Crowns on that same Order.
- Imperial Grant, Imperial Kiln, Imperial Priority token and Imperial Audience are each gained only once.
## End-game Exhibition
At game end, choose **up to 5** of your undelivered ceramics of Standard Quality or better to exhibit.
| **Quality** | **VP each** |
|:-|:-:|
| Standard | **2** |
| Fine | **3** |
| Masterpiece | **5** |
Then choose **3 of your exhibited ceramics** as the featured collection:
- +3 VP if the 3 have **3 different Shapes**;
- +3 VP if the 3 have **3 different Glazes**.
If you exhibit fewer than 3 ceramics, you cannot gain either diversity bonus.
Flawed ceramics cannot be exhibited.
# Kiln Tradition Abilities
Kiln Player Boards use abbreviated reminder text. The rules in this section give the full effects.
## Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection
Once per round, when you complete an Order using a **Masterpiece with Celadon Glaze and Plain Decoration**, gain **4 VP**.
The Order itself does not need to require Masterpiece, Celadon or Plain; the delivered ceramic must actually have those attributes.
## Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage
Once per round, when you complete an Order showing **at least 1 Crown**, gain **2 Coins and 1 VP**.
## Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle from Fire
Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated but before Quality is assigned, you may choose one of your ceramics whose Actual Heat differs from its Preferred Heat by **exactly 1**.
Set its Actual Heat equal to its Preferred Heat and change its Decoration to **Crackle** without paying the Crackle cost.
## Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production
Once per round, when resolving a Potter’s Wheel action, after you form a **Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer**, you may form **one additional vessel of the same Shape** at no Clay cost.
This additional vessel does not count against that action's normal vessel limit.
## Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation
Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated but before Quality is assigned, you may pay **1 Wood** to adjust the Actual Heat of one of your ceramics by **+1 or −1**.
### Ability Timing
- If several players resolve abilities in the same window, resolve them beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise.
- Ge and Jun act after Actual Heat is calculated and before Quality is assigned.
- Ru and Guan trigger while completing Orders.
- Ding triggers during its Potter’s Wheel action.
# End of the Game and Scoring
The game ends after Round 5 Cleanup.
Score:
1. all VP recorded during play, including Orders, Kiln Tradition abilities, **Imperial Audience** and VP from Crowns gained beyond Recognition 4;
2. **1 VP for each Advanced Tech** you own;
3. the end-game Exhibition;
4. **1 VP per 3 Coins remaining**, maximum **5 VP**.
The player with the most VP wins.
### Tie Breakers
1. Farther along Imperial Recognition.
2. More Crown icons printed across completed Orders.
3. More Masterpiece ceramics delivered or exhibited.
4. If still tied, share victory.
# General Clarifications
- Clay, Wood and Coins are limited by the supply. If a supply is empty, receive only what remains.
- Vessel cards are not a hard gameplay limit. If all 10 cards of a Shape are in use, use a suitable proxy until a matching card becomes available.
- Players may not trade resources, Orders, Techs or ceramics.
- Shaped, Glazed and Finished ceramics remain between rounds and keep all recorded attributes until a rule explicitly changes, delivers or discards them.
- Uncompleted Orders and unused ceramics have no end-game penalty.
- If the Main Order deck is empty when a card is needed, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
- If the Fire deck is empty when a card is needed, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
- If an Advanced Tech discipline cannot refill its display to 2, leave the display partially empty.

# Appendix D — Full Firing Sequence
If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, **skip this phase and do not reveal a Fire card**.
1. **Pre-firing abilities.** Resolve Test Pieces and similar effects before Contributions are chosen.
2. **Choose Contributions.** Every player with at least 1 ceramic in the Shared Kiln or an Imperial Kiln secretly chooses Bank, Tend or Stoke and must be able to pay its Wood cost. Fuel Ledger may add its hidden Wood commitment.
3. **Reveal and pay.** Reveal all Contribution cards and committed Wood simultaneously, then pay all costs.
4. **Determine Base Heat.** Start at 2, add all final Contribution modifiers, then clamp to 0–5.
5. **Kiln Yard Shifu adjustment.** In **First Player order**, each player who used a Kiln Yard Shifu this round may move **1 of their ceramics in the Shared Kiln** to an empty active space in a **neighbouring heat zone** (High ↔ Middle ↔ Low). A Kiln Furniture tile moves with its ceramic. Imperial Kiln ceramics cannot be moved this way.
6. **Reveal Fire.** Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier to Base Heat. This is Global Heat and is not capped.
7. **Calculate Actual Heat.** Shared-Kiln ceramics add their zone modifier; Imperial Kiln ceramics initially equal Global Heat. A ceramic carrying Kiln Furniture uses a zone modifier of 0.
8. **Heat-adjustment abilities.** In First Player order, resolve Jun, Ge and any other abilities that occur after Actual Heat is calculated and before Quality is assigned.
9. **Assign Quality.** Compare final Actual Heat with Preferred Heat.
10. **After-Quality abilities.** In First Player order, resolve Protective Saggars, Second Firing and similar effects. If you control multiple abilities in this window, choose their order.
11. **Salvage a Flawed ceramic, if desired.** Each player may discard **1 ceramic still Flawed from this firing** to gain **2 Coins**. Return its Vessel card to the matching Shape supply.
12. **Finish firing.** Move all remaining fired ceramics to their owners' Finished Ceramics areas. Empty all Shared Kiln and Imperial Kiln spaces. Return Kiln Furniture tiles to their owners, discard the main Fire card face up, and return all Contribution cards.

# Appendix E — Quick Reference
## Round Sequence
1. **Start:** Rounds 2–5 discard the 3 leftmost Main Orders, slide the remaining 2 left and refill the **5-card display**; ready abilities.
2. **Work:** First Player clockwise; place workers until everyone passes.
3. **Fire:** Contributions → Base Heat → **Kiln Yard Shifu** → Fire → Actual Heat → abilities → Quality.
4. **Orders:** reverse Work order; one Order per opportunity; repeat until a full circuit completes **no Orders**.
5. **Cleanup:** return workers; trim Orders to 3; if the game continues, pass First Player and advance the round.
## Action Summary
