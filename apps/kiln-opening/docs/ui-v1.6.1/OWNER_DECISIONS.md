# V1.6.1 UI: owner decisions

Answers given by the owner on 2026-10-15 to the wireframe sign-off questions ([wireframes](./kiln-opening-wireframes.html)). They bind the production UI built in phases 4–6 of the [implementation plan](../V1.6_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

## Guiding principle: show only what the table shows

**The UI shows only information a player could see playing the physical board game. It adds no extra analysis.**

- No Fire-card odds or cards-left counts.
- No previews of the Quality each Fire card would give.
- No heat ladder or heat ledger analysis tables, and no "before Fire" heat predictions.
- The engine may still compute these values for the computer player, but no screen shows them.

Things the table does show, the UI shows too:

- the Kiln Die;
- the Base Heat marker after Contributions are revealed;
- the Fire card once it is turned;
- each ceramic's position, markers and modifiers;
- the Quality recorded on a fired ceramic;
- the public history in the game log.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Where does your board live? | **All Kiln Player Boards live in one board area.** It shows your own board by default. Tabs on the board's top edge switch to each other player's board. A **Your workshop** button returns to yours. Clicking a seat in the top bar opens that player's board. |
| D2 | Opponent detail | The seat chips along the top of the screen are the public opponent strips; that level is right. Full boards are reached through D1. |
| D3 | Firing prominence | **Firing takes over the main column** during the Firing Phase. It shows the step rail, the heat equation (Kiln Die + Contributions = Base Heat; Base Heat + Fire = Global Heat), the kiln and the Fire discard, and nothing beyond the physical game's information (see the principle above). |
| D4 | Fire-card odds | **No odds.** Players can see which Fire cards have been discarded, at any time. That is all the Fire-deck information shown. |
| D5 | Phone tabs | **Table / Kiln / Workshop / Orders** is right. The **game log is a dropdown** available at every screen size, not a tab or section. |
| D6 | Chinese terms | The glossary terms are approved. |
| D7 | Second personal-action space | **No "lock" or "unlock" in any player-facing text.** The second Form & Decorate or Glaze & Load space is something you **add by learning a Tech**. Before you have it, it reads "Learn a Form & Decorate Tech to add this space" / 习得坯作技艺即添此工位. Once you have it, it reads "Added by ⟨Tech⟩" / 由「⟨技艺⟩」添设. Internal identifiers (`unlocked`, `PERSONAL_SPACE_LOCKED`, `unlocks`) are unchanged. |
| D8 | Shared Kiln layout (2026-10-15) | **Low at the top, High at the bottom.** The fire burns at the bottom of a wood-fired kiln, so the High (+1) row sits beside the firebox at the bottom. Middle (0) is in the middle, and the two Low (−1) spaces sit centred under the dome at the top, following the kiln's shape. This is layout only: zone modifiers, space counts and covered spaces per player count are unchanged. |
| D9 | Internal IDs (2026-10-15) | **Never show internal IDs to players.** Ceramic IDs (`c4`, `c18`…), worker IDs and other engine identifiers do not appear in anything a player sees, because a physical Vessel card has no number. A ceramic is recognised by its picture (Shape, Decoration, Glaze tint, owner's colour ring, Quality badge, Crackle marker) and is described in words in labels, choices, the game log, error messages and screen-reader text, e.g. "Lin's Celadon Vase, Carved · Fine", adding where it is ("in High 2", "in your workshop") only when two would otherwise read the same. Printed card numbers such as Order S01 or O33 stay, because they are on the physical cards. |
| D10 | Full rules on demand (2026-10-15; narrowed 2026-10-17) | **Clicking, tapping or focusing a Tech tile, Kiln Player Board, location, Order or Imperial Recognition milestone opens its rules details**, the way a player at the table would check the rulebook. Beside a form option the trigger is a small **"Full rules"** button (完整规则). **The panel shows only two sections** (owner review, 2026-10-17): the **full rule** word for word from the rulebook in force (V1.6.2 since 2026-10-17; EN or 中文), and the **clarifications** for that component, as plain text. It shows no card text and no card facts (the card already carries them), and no source labels. The clarifications leave out general rules that apply to every component alike (for example "abilities are optional") and interactions the component's owner can never meet (for example Crackle on the Ru board: only Ge makes Crackle ceramics). This is rules reference, not game analysis: no advice, odds or previews (D3/D4 still apply). |
| D11 | Tech discipline name (2026-10-15) | **The "Workshop" Tech discipline is called "Form & Decorate" in English**, matching the personal location whose second space it adds: "Form & Decorate Techs", "Learn a Form & Decorate Tech to add this space". The other disciplines keep their names (Glazing & Loading, Firing). Chinese keeps the approved 坯作 (D6). Wording only: the internal ID `workshop` is unchanged. The checked-in rulebook is updated too (amendment A2 in the audit). |
| D12 | Seat chips (2026-10-17, owner review of phase B) | **On desktop and tablet the seat chips sit in the jump bar, after the section buttons** (Table, Orders, Recognition & Techs, Kiln, Workshops); they stay on that line when they fit and take their own line otherwise. On phones they stay in the top bar. A chip shows the First Player badge, name, Kiln (nothing before Kiln selection, never "no Kiln yet"), VP, **Clay, Wood and Coins as icons with counts** (like the hand's card back), the hand count and the acting dot. There is **no "computer" pill**; a computer seat is said in the chip's accessible name only. |
| D13 | Kiln selection (2026-10-17) | The Kiln selection window **does not list the choosing order**; its prompt still says Kilns are chosen in reverse turn order, and the seat chips show who is choosing. |
| D14 | Placing workers (2026-10-17) | **Workers are placed on the action spaces themselves**: each table location card and each personal space on your board has its Place buttons. The decision pane has **no worker or location picker**, and **during your work turn it is hidden until a placement is chosen** (owner review item 2): the table takes the full width on desktop and tablet, and phones show no bottom sheet. Pressing Place brings the pane up with that space's summary, its choices and Confirm (on phones the sheet opens). Clear hides it again, and so does your next work turn after Confirm; in between, the pane shows what comes next (a Commission Market or Guild Hall step, the after-action window, waiting for the other players). While it is hidden, a slim line above the table says "Your turn: place a worker · 3 left"; an ability that may come before the worker action (Imperial Priority, and in V1.6.2 the Colour Samples swap) has a small button after it ("Or first: [Imperial Priority]") that opens the pane for it, and the ability's form has a "Back to placing a worker" button that hides the pane again. Every other decision keeps the pane. **At the Guild Hall** the player is taken to the Tech display, which becomes the chooser: face-up Techs (Apprentice) or "Inspect the deck" per discipline (Shifu) are toggles, and Techs the player cannot buy are greyed out with the reason. In V1.6.2 the Shifu's takes that follow the inspection may be chosen there too: the face-up Techs are toggles again for the first and the second take, while the inspected Techs stay in the pane, seen only by that player. |
| D15 | Rules links (2026-10-17) | No "Full rules" button in the Kiln selection window or the Shifu's Guild Hall inspection, and **no rules-details link on any action space** (table cards or personal spaces) **or owned Tech tile on a Kiln Player Board** (D19). Tech tiles on the Tech display (when not a Guild Hall choice), Kiln boards, Orders and milestones keep theirs (D10). |
| D16 | Kiln section (2026-10-17) | The Shared Kiln (and the Table section's small kiln) draws **only the active spaces** for the player count, rows centred: 4 players 2/3/3, 3 players 2/2/2, 2 players 1/1/2. The "Last firing" recap is removed; the Kiln section shows **this round's Kiln Die** and a **"Fire discard" button** that lists every discarded Fire card, oldest first (no count of cards left). |
| D17 | Other players' turns (2026-10-17) | A **toast** shows what another player did on one turn, in the game log's words (at most 4 lines, then "and 2 more lines in the game log"), and closes by itself **3 seconds** after it appears or last changes (hover or focus pauses it; × or Escape closes it). **One toast at a time, in turn order, at the top right** of the game screen (owner review item 3): below the top bar and the jump bar with its seat chips, and below the line that says "Your turn: place a worker" while the decision pane is hidden (D14); on phones and in short landscape it runs across the top of the table instead. The table makes room for it and keeps its place on screen, so the toast is drawn over nothing (never over the Log button, the seat chips, the decision pane's Confirm or a table control: what its room takes from the top of the table is scrolled out of view, not covered, and scrolling brings it back). The jump bar is a row of its own above the table, which never scrolls under it. The other players' turns queue as they arrive, with no limit, and each shows for 3 seconds, counted only while it can be seen and is not paused (not while the page is hidden, or while a phone's open decision sheet leaves it no room): in a 3-player game, after your turn, Computer 1's turn, then Computer 2's. The viewer's own moves and hidden information never appear, and nothing is replayed after a reload, a reconnect, or a time with the page hidden or offline. |
| D18 | Hands section (2026-10-17) | The table's "Hands: counts are public, cards are not" section is removed; the seat chips show each hand count. |
| D19 | Tech tiles and the hand (2026-10-17, owner review items 1, 4 and 5) | **Every Tech tile shows its printed short text, with the timing clause in bold**, as on the Tech display. **In the Shifu's Guild Hall inspection** ("Take a Tech") each Tech, inspected or face up, is such a tile, with its price and discipline, and stays a single choice (a radio); one the Shifu cannot take is greyed out with the reason, its timing clause still bold. In V1.6.2 the price is the printed cost of every take, and the Shifu takes one Tech at a time: after the first, another of a different discipline or none ("Take another Tech, or stop"); after the second, only the bottom order of the inspected Techs left is asked ("Return the inspected Techs"). The window has no "Only you can see" pill and no "Full rules" buttons (D15). **An owned Tech on a Kiln Player Board** shows its name, its state this round (Ready or Used this round; Ongoing for a Tech that is not once per round) and its short text; the tile opens no rules details. **The table's Your hand row** has no "Only you can see" pill. |
| D20 | Phone tab and own hand (2026-10-18) | On phones the viewer's **work turn opens on the Table tab** (workers are placed from the table, D14), not the Workshop tab. The viewer's own Kiln Player Board shows the hand count **without** the "Only you can see" pill, like the table's hand row (D19). |
| D21 | Free actions (2026-10-30, revision 5) | **Free actions are never a step of their own, and a turn with nothing extra costs one Confirm.** Before placing, the slim turn line (D14) reads "Your turn: place a worker · 3 left" followed by **Free actions** chips for what the viewer can do right now: Deliver ⟨Order⟩ for each held Order they can complete, Sell Flawed (+2 Coins), Imperial Priority, and the Colour Samples swap (not a free action, but it shares the before/after-action timing). It replaces D14's "Or first:" buttons. A chip opens the pane with that action's form; after its Confirm the pane hides again for placing. The same actions sit on the pieces: a Deliver badge on a completable Order in the hand, Sell 2 on the viewer's Flawed ceramics, Use on the Imperial Priority token. Nothing has to be dismissed: placing a worker is how the turn moves on. **The worker action's last Confirm is a split button: Place · end turn** (the default, Enter) / 派工并结束回合, and **Place, then free actions** (Shift+Enter) / 派工后做自由行动. When the action's last step brings an Order into the hand (the Commission Market's Select, the Magistrate's Hall Shifu's Crown Order, a Colour Samples Select) the choice sits on that step instead: Reserve · end turn / 承接并结束回合 and Reserve, then free actions / 承接后做自由行动, with a private hint "You can deliver this now" / 现在就能交付 when the chosen Order matches one of the viewer's Finished ceramics. After "then free actions", each free action's Confirm offers ⟨Action⟩ · end turn beside ⟨Action⟩, and End turn stays available. **The Order Phase** is the post-firing free-action window (owner, 2026-10-30): its pane is the same list of free actions, with Deliver · done and Done; a player with nothing to do is skipped automatically, as before. The server ends the turn in the same command (an optional "end the turn afterwards" field on the confirming command), so other players see one change; no window opens or closes on the hand's content, and the hints use only the viewer's own view. |

D7 changes wording only, not rules. The owner's own rulebook edits of 2026-10-16 brought the printed rulebook in line: the physical "Lock tokens" are now **Cover tokens**, and a Tech "removes the Cover token" from a second personal space (see the audit, "Owner wording edits"). The digital UI has no such token.
