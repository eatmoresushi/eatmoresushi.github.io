# V1.2.7 Tech tile and hover wording — owner amendment, 2026-09-20

These short reminders replace the Tech wording in the original component draft. Tile faces and hover previews use the same text. Clicked tiles use the detailed Tech source, with the owner-confirmed Kiln Tending correction to 1 Clay or 1 Wood. Tech abilities are optional unless explicitly required; any stated use cost must be paid. Advanced-Tech Once per round footers remain visible.

### Prepared Clay
After each Materials Yard action: pay 1 more clay cost → form 1 vessel

### White Slip
After each Potter’s Wheel action: pay 1 coin → give 1 vessel just formed White + Plain

### Rapid Drying
After each Glaze & Decoration action: pay 1 Wood → load 1 ceramic just glazed

### Kiln Tending
After each Kiln Yard action: gain 1 Clay or 1 Wood.

### Large Throwing Wheel
During a Potter’s Wheel action forming at least 1 Vase or Censer → reduce the action’s total Clay cost by 2, minimum 0. This stacks with the Shifu discount.

### Measuring Calipers
After forming a vessel, if you have another Shaped or Glazed vessel of a different Shape → gain 2 Coins.

### Standardised Moulds
After you form a vessel, if you have another Shaped or Glazed vessel of the same Shape → gain 2 Coins.

### Drying Frames
After a Potter’s Wheel action: pay the Decoration cost → give 1 Shaped vessel formed by that action any Glaze + any Decoration.

### Reworking Table
During a Glaze & Decoration action, change the Shape of 1 Shaped vessel being glazed to any other Shape.

### Glaze Palette
Immediately before loading 1 of your Glazed ceramics, change it to any Glaze at no cost. Its Shape and Decoration stay unchanged.

### Carving Knives
One Carved Decoration you apply costs 0 Coins.

### Seal Stamps
One Impressed Decoration you apply costs 0 Coins.

### Crackle Slips
One Crackle Decoration you apply costs 0 Coins.

### Colour Samples
When acquired: Immediately make 1 selection
Once per round: Replace 1 reservation choice during a Commission Market action with selection.

### Protective Saggars
After Quality is assigned: pay 1 Wood → improve 1 of your ceramics in this firing: Flawed → Standard or Standard → Fine.

### Fuel Ledger
Secretly commit 1 extra Wood with your Contribution. Reveal and pay it with your Contribution to make Bank −2 or Stoke +2.

### Test Pieces
Before Contributions, if you have a ceramic in this firing: pay 1 Wood → privately look at the top Fire card. Return it to the top without showing it.

### Second Firing
After Quality is assigned: choose 1 of your Flawed or Standard ceramics in this firing → reveal 1 extra Fire card and recalculate only its Actual Heat and Quality using the same Base Heat and kiln position.

### Kiln Furniture
When loading 1 of your ceramics into a High or Low Shared Kiln space: place this tile beneath it → its zone modifier is 0 for this firing, even if moved.
