# KILN OPENING 开窑 — Component Text Draft


## Starting Techs


### Prepared Clay

**Once during each Materials Yard action**


After gaining resources, you may form **1 vessel of any Shape** by paying its **normal Clay cost + 1 Clay**.


### White Slip

**Once during each Potter’s Wheel action**


After forming, you may give **1 vessel formed by this action** **White Glaze + Plain Decoration**. Pay the Plain Decoration cost.


### Rapid Drying

**Once during each Glaze & Decoration action**


After glazing, you may pay **1 Wood** to load **1 ceramic glazed by this action** into an empty active Shared Kiln space or your empty Imperial Kiln, if gained.


### Kiln Tending

**Once during each Kiln Yard action**


After loading at least **1 ceramic**, gain **1 Clay and 1 Wood**.


## Forming Advanced Techs


### Large Throwing Wheel

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


During a **Potter’s Wheel** action that forms at least **1 Vase or Censer**, reduce the action’s **total Clay cost by 2**, minimum 0. This stacks with the Shifu discount.


### Measuring Calipers

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


After you form a vessel, if you have another **Shaped or Glazed vessel of a different Shape**, gain **2 Coins**.


### Standardised Moulds

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


After you form a vessel, if you have another **Shaped or Glazed vessel of the same Shape**, gain **2 Coins**.


### Drying Frames

**Acquisition: 3 Coins** · **Once per round**


After a **Potter’s Wheel** action, choose **1 Shaped vessel formed by that action**. Immediately give it **any Glaze and Decoration**, paying the Decoration cost.


### Reworking Table

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


During a **Glaze & Decoration** action, you may change the Shape of **1 Shaped vessel being glazed** to any other Shape. Exchange its Vessel card. **No additional Clay is paid.**


## Glazing & Decoration Advanced Techs


### Glaze Palette

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


Immediately before loading **1 of your Glazed ceramics**, you may change it to **any Glaze at no cost**. Its Shape and Decoration stay unchanged.


### Carving Knives

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


One **Carved Decoration** you apply costs **0 Coins**.


### Seal Stamps

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


One **Impressed Decoration** you apply costs **0 Coins**.


### Crackle Slips

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


One **Crackle Decoration** you apply costs **0 Coins**.


### Colour Samples

**Acquisition: 2 Coins**

**Selection:** Privately look at the top **3 Main Orders** (or as many as remain). Reserve **1 of these or 1 face-up Main Order**. Discard unreserved looked-at Orders; update the public display normally.

**When acquired:** Immediately make 1 selection

**Once per round:** Replace **1 reservation choice** during a Commission Market action with selection.


## Firing Advanced Techs


### Protective Saggars

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


After Quality is assigned, you may pay **1 Wood** to improve **1 of your ceramics from this firing**: **Flawed → Standard** or **Standard → Fine**. This cannot create a Masterpiece.


### Fuel Ledger

**Acquisition: 3 Coins** · **When choosing Bank or Stoke**


You may secretly commit **1 extra Wood** with your Contribution. Reveal and pay it with your Contribution to make **Bank −2** or **Stoke +2**.


### Test Pieces

**Acquisition: 3 Coins** · **Once per round**


Before Contributions are chosen, if you have a ceramic in this firing, you may pay **1 Wood** to privately look at the **top Fire card**. Return it to the top without showing it.


### Second Firing

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


After Quality is assigned, choose **1 of your Flawed or Standard ceramics from this firing**. Reveal an extra Fire card and recalculate its Actual Heat and Quality using the **same Base Heat and kiln position**. Ignore previous Actual Heat adjustments. Keep the new Quality, **even if worse**, then discard the extra card. Unused firing abilities may still resolve at their normal timing.


### Kiln Furniture

**Acquisition: 2 Coins** · **Once per round**


When loading **1 of your ceramics into a High or Low Shared Kiln space**, you may place this tile beneath it. Its zone modifier is **0 for this firing**, even if moved. Move this tile with it; return the tile after firing.


## Kiln Player Boards


### Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection

**Once per round**


When you complete an Order using a **Masterpiece with Celadon Glaze and Plain Decoration**, gain **4 VP**.


### Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage

**Once per round**


When you complete a **Crown Order**, gain **2 Coins and 1 VP**.


### Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle Mastery

**When assigning Quality:** Your **Crackle ceramics with Heat Difference 2** are **Fine instead of Standard**.

**Once per round:** When completing an Order, you may treat **1 of your Crackle ceramics used** as having **any one Decoration** for all that Order’s Decoration requirements. Its actual Decoration stays Crackle.


### Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production

**Once per round**


During a **Potter’s Wheel** action, after forming a **Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer**, you may pay **1 Clay** to form **1 extra vessel of the same Shape**.

The extra vessel does not count towards the action limit or Shifu discount. It may be chosen for White Slip or Drying Frames.


### Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation

**Once per round**


After Actual Heat is calculated, but before Quality is assigned, you may pay **1 Wood** to adjust **1 of your ceramics’ Actual Heat by +1 or −1**.
