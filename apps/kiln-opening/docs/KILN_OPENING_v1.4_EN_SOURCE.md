# KILN OPENING 开窑 v1.4 — Player Rulebook

**2–4 players | 90–120 minutes | Ages 12+**  
*Build the workshop. Share the kiln. Master the craft.*

A medium-weight worker-placement game inspired by the celebrated ceramic traditions of Song China.

# Game Overview

In **Kiln Opening**, each player directs a ceramic workshop inspired by one of five renowned kiln traditions. Your Shifu and Apprentices gather materials, form vessels, apply Decorations, glaze ceramics, compete for Orders and specialised knowledge, and load ceramics into a shared kiln whose final firing is influenced by every participating workshop.
**Worker placement determines what your workshop prepares. The shared firing determines what that preparation becomes.**
The game lasts exactly **5 rounds**.

# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Ceramic | One Vessel card as it moves through Workshop, Loaded, Fired and Delivered states. |
| Workshop ceramic | A formed, unfired and unloaded vessel in your workshop. It has a Shape and one Decoration, starting with Plain. |
| Apprentice | A normal worker. At action spaces it receives the Apprentice effect. |
| Shifu | A player's master worker. It receives Shifu effects and may be placed on a fully occupied action location. |
| Starting Tech | A workshop ability chosen during Setup. It does not count toward the 2-Advanced-Tech limit. |
| Advanced Tech | A permanent workshop technique acquired at the Craft Academy. A player may own at most 2. |
| Tech effect | An effect printed on a Starting or Advanced Tech. A Tech effect is **not a worker action** unless explicitly stated otherwise. |
| Decoration | How a vessel's surface is treated: Plain, Carved, Impressed or Painted. Plain is free when formed; the other Decorations replace Plain. |
| Glaze | White, Celadon, Grey-Green or Moon White. Each has a Preferred Heat. |
| Order | A Starting Order or Main Order showing ceramic requirements and rewards. |
| Crown Order | Any Order showing at least 1 Crown icon. |
| Reserved Order | An Order in your hand, protected from other players. |
| Imperial Kiln | A private one-ceramic kiln gained at Imperial Recognition 2. Its ceramic uses Global Heat with no zone modifier. |
| Imperial Priority | A once-per-game token gained at Imperial Recognition 3. It can glaze and load one of your workshop ceramics into your empty Imperial Kiln outside a Kiln Yard action. |
| Preferred Heat | White 1, Celadon 2, Grey-Green 3, Moon White 4. |
| Base Heat | Start at 2, add Contribution modifiers, then clamp to 0–5. |
| Global Heat | Base Heat plus the Fire-card modifier. Not capped. |
| Actual Heat | Global Heat plus the ceramic's kiln-space modifier and any personal Heat modifier. |
| Heat Difference | Absolute difference between Actual Heat and Preferred Heat; determines Quality. |
| Crackle | A permanent Ge-only firing property. Ge changes one Standard ceramic to Fine and gives it Crackle. For Orders, it may count as any one Decoration, but not a different Glaze. |

# Setup

1. Place the Central Action Board and Shared Kiln in the centre. Use the following active Shared Kiln spaces:
   - **2 players:** **1 High / 2 Middle / 1 Low — 4 spaces**. Cover 2 High spaces and 2 Low space.
   - **3 players:** **2 High / 2 Middle / 2 Low — 6 spaces**. Cover 1 High space and 1 Low space
   - **4 players:** **3 High / 2 Middle / 3 Low — 8 spaces**. Use all spaces.
2. Shuffle the separate **8-card Starting Order deck** and **48-card Main Order deck**.
3. Deal each player **1 Starting Order and 1 Main Order**. These form the player's starting hand and are secret.
4. Return undealt Starting Orders to the box. Reshuffle the remaining Main Order deck and reveal **6 face-up Main Orders** from left to right.
5. Separate the 15 Advanced Techs into Forming, Decoration & Glazing and Firing decks of 5. Shuffle each discipline and reveal **2 face-up tiles from each**.
6. Shuffle the Fire deck and place it face down beside a face-up discard pile.
7. Place resources, Vessel cards, Imperial Kilns, Heat markers and Crackle markers within reach. Set the **+2 Stoke** and **−2 Bank** cards beside the Tech displays; they are gained only by the player who acquires Fuel Ledger.
8. Each player chooses a colour and takes:
   - 1 Shifu;
   - 3 Apprentices;
   - 1 Imperial Recognition marker;
   - 1 Bank, 1 Tend and 1 Stoke Contribution card;
   - that colour's Imperial Priority token, placing it on Recognition space 3.
9. Randomly choose the First Player.
10. In reverse turn order, each player chooses an available Kiln Player Board.
11. Each player receives **2 Clay, 2 Wood and 4 Coins**.
12. Each player chooses **1 Starting Tech**. Starting Techs are public information.
13. Place all Imperial Recognition markers on space 0 and the Round marker on Round 1.

# Round Structure

| Phase | Name | Summary |
|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | Start of Round | Rotate Main Orders; refill displays; ready abilities. |
| 2 | Work Phase | Place every worker, then resolve end-of-Work-Phase abilities. |
| 3 | Firing Phase | Contributions → Base Heat → Shifu Heat adjustment → Fire → Actual Heat → Quality. |
| 4 | Order Phase | Complete public or held Orders one at a time. |
| 5 | Cleanup | Return workers; trim Order hands; pass First Player. |

## Phase 1 — Start of Round

In Rounds 2–5:

1. Discard the **2 leftmost face-up Main Orders** to the Main Order discard pile.
2. Slide the remaining 4 Main Orders left.
3. Reveal 2 new Main Orders, restoring the display to 6.

Then ready all once-per-round abilities. The First Player begins the Work Phase.

## Phase 2 — Work Phase

Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player must place **1 available worker** at a legal action location and resolve its action immediately.

Continue until **every player has placed all 4 workers** and completed their actions and associated effects. Resolve end-of-Work-Phase abilities, including **Glaze Palette**, in First Player order. Then proceed to the Firing Phase.

Each player therefore has **20 worker placements per game, including 5 Shifu placements**.

- Multiple spaces are available for each capped action.
- A worker may use any unoccupied space at that location.
- A **Shifu** follows normal placement rules except that it may be placed on a fully occupied action location. Multiple Shifu may overfill the same location.
- A worker placement must perform at least **1 instance of that location's main action**. “Up to 2” means 1 or 2, never 0.
- All mandatory costs must be affordable when the action is taken, after applying available discounts or waivers.
- **You may not pass during the Work Phase.** Paid Work has no capacity limit or resource cost, so it is always a legal placement.

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Gain **3 resources** in any combination of Clay and Wood. |
| **Shifu** | Gain **4 resources** in any combination of Clay and Wood. Then you may pay **1 Coin** to gain **+1 Clay and +1 Wood**. |

### Potter's Wheel

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Pay the Clay cost to form **1 vessel**. |
| **Shifu** | Form up to **2 vessels**. If you form 2, reduce their **total Clay cost by 1**. |

#### Shapes and Forming Costs

| Shape | Clay Cost |
|---|:---:|
| Bowl | 1 |
| Plate | 1 |
| Brush Washer | 1 |
| Vase | 2 |
| Censer | 2 |

Vessel cards are not a hard gameplay limit. If all matching cards are in use, use a suitable proxy.

#### Default Plain

**Every vessel starts with Plain Decoration at no Coin cost when it is formed.** This applies whether it is formed by a worker action, a Tech effect or a Kiln Tradition.

Plain is a Decoration for all game rules, including **any**, **same** and **different Decorations**. Applying its automatic Plain Decoration does not count as taking a Decoration Workshop action or replacing Plain with another Decoration.

The production pipeline is:

> **Form (Plain) → optionally replace Plain at the Decoration Workshop → Glaze & Load → Fire**

A Plain vessel can skip the Decoration Workshop and proceed directly to the Kiln Yard.

#### Ceramic States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Workshop** | Formed, unfired and unloaded. Records Shape and one Decoration: Plain by default, or Carved, Impressed or Painted after specialisation. |
| **Loaded** | Also records Glaze and occupies a Shared Kiln or Imperial Kiln space. |
| **Fired** | Also records Quality and is kept in your Finished Ceramics area. |
| **Delivered** | Used for a completed Order; it cannot be used again or exhibited. |

A workshop ceramic has no Glaze yet. Specialising its Decoration does not change its Workshop state. Loaded, Fired and Delivered ceramics are not in your workshop for Tech requirements.

### Decoration Workshop

Pay **2 Coins per vessel** to replace **Plain** with **Carved, Impressed or Painted** on your workshop ceramics. Choose a Decoration separately for each vessel.

Plain, Carved, Impressed and Painted represent different decoration techniques or surface treatments. For gameplay, they are collectively called **Decorations**.

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Replace Plain with **Carved, Impressed or Painted** on **1 of your workshop ceramics**; pay **2 Coins**. |
| **Shifu** | Replace Plain with **Carved, Impressed or Painted** on up to **2 of your workshop ceramics**. **One Decoration applied by this action costs 0 Coins**; pay **2 Coins** for the other, before any other waiver. |

#### Decoration Cost

**Plain is automatic and free when forming; it is not applied at this location.** Replacing Plain with **Carved, Impressed or Painted costs 2 Coins** before discounts or waivers.

Only your **Plain workshop ceramics** may be worked here. Each specialised Decoration replaces Plain; a ceramic has exactly one actual Decoration. Once Plain is replaced, its Decoration cannot be changed again unless an ability explicitly allows it.

### Kiln Yard

A Kiln Yard action **glazes and immediately loads** your workshop ceramics. **Plain ceramics are eligible**, as are Carved, Impressed and Painted ceramics.

For each ceramic processed:

1. choose any one Glaze;
2. pay the **1-Coin Glazing cost**;
3. load it into an empty active Shared Kiln space or your empty Imperial Kiln, if gained.

You must have a legal empty destination and be able to pay before glazing. Glazing and loading are one operation: there is no **Glazed but unloaded** workshop state. Each active kiln space holds one ceramic; Shifu worker overfill does not increase kiln capacity.

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Glaze and load **1 of your workshop ceramics**. |
| **Shifu** | Glaze and load up to **2 of your workshop ceramics**. Place your Shifu on **1 ceramic loaded by this action**, in either the Shared Kiln or your Imperial Kiln. |

#### Kiln Yard Shifu Heat Control

**After Base Heat is determined, before the Fire card is revealed:** you may replace your Shifu with a **+1 or −1 Heat marker** on the ceramic carrying it.

- The adjustment is optional and costs no Wood. If you decline, leave the ceramic unadjusted.
- The marked ceramic must be one **loaded by that Shifu action**. You cannot mark an earlier load or a ceramic loaded by Imperial Priority.
- The marker changes **only that ceramic's Actual Heat**, not Base Heat or Global Heat.
- The marker remains fixed for this firing, including any Second Firing.
- Set a removed Shifu aside; it remains used until Cleanup. Remove the Heat marker after the firing.

#### Glaze Cost and Preferred Heat

Applying a Glaze costs **1 Coin per ceramic**, including when glazing through Rapid Drying or Imperial Priority. Those effects include this payment; it is not paid a second time.

| Glaze | Preferred Heat |
|---|:---:|
| White | 1 |
| Celadon | 2 |
| Grey-Green | 3 |
| Moon White | 4 |

After loading, a ceramic's Glaze cannot change unless an ability explicitly allows it. **Glaze Palette** changes a Glaze without paying another Glazing cost.

### Commission Market

For each reservation, choose one:

- reserve **1 face-up Main Order**. Remove it, slide every Order to its right one position left, then reveal the top Main Order into the rightmost empty position; or
- reserve the **top card of the Main Order deck without looking at it first**.

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Reserve **1 Main Order**. Then gain **1 Clay, 1 Wood or 1 Coin**. |
| **Shifu** | Reserve up to **2 Main Orders**, resolving each separately. After each reservation, gain **1 Clay, 1 Wood or 1 Coin**. |

- A Shifu must reserve at least 1 Order but may stop after the first.
- There is no hand limit during the round.
- During Cleanup, discard down to **3 Orders**.
- Orders in hand are secret. The number of Orders in each player's hand is public.

### Craft Academy

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Pay the printed Coin cost and take **1 face-up Advanced Tech**. |
| **Shifu** | Choose 1 discipline and look at the top 2 Techs of that deck, or as many as remain. Then take either 1 face-up Advanced Tech from any discipline or 1 of the revealed Techs, paying **1 Coin less**, minimum 0. Put revealed Techs not taken on the bottom of that discipline's deck. |

- Refill a discipline's display to 2 after a tile is taken, if possible.
- A player may own at most **2 Advanced Techs**.
- An Apprentice placement is legal only if you can afford at least **1 face-up Advanced Tech**.
- A Shifu placement is legal only if you can afford at least **1 face-up Advanced Tech after its 1-Coin discount**. This guarantees a legal fallback if none of the searched Techs can be taken.
- A newly acquired Advanced Tech may be used in the same round if its timing permits.
- Resolve any **when acquired** effect immediately.

### Paid Work

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | Gain **2 Coins**. |
| **Shifu** | Gain **4 Coins**. |

### Imperial Court

The Imperial Court has no shared capacity limit.

| Worker | Effect |
|---|---|
| Any worker | Pay **4 Coins** to advance your Imperial Recognition by **1 step**. |

- The Imperial Court may advance Recognition **0 → 1, 1 → 2 or 2 → 3**.
- Resolve the reward of the Recognition space reached normally.
- The Imperial Court **cannot advance you to Recognition 4**.
- Shifu and Apprentices resolve this action identically.

## Phase 3 — Firing Phase

If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, skip the Firing Phase and do not reveal a Fire card.

### Contribution Cards

Every player has:

| Contribution | Wood | Heat |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Bank the Fire | 1 | −1 |
| Tend the Fire | 0 | 0 |
| Stoke the Fire | 1 | +1 |

- A player participates in Contributions if they have at least 1 ceramic in either the Shared Kiln or their Imperial Kiln. Every participating player chooses **exactly 1 Contribution card**; players with no loaded ceramics do not contribute.
- **Fuel Ledger:** If you own Fuel Ledger, **+2 Stoke** and **−2 Bank** are additional Contribution choices. Each costs **2 Wood**. You still choose **exactly 1 Contribution card per firing**.
- Contributions are chosen secretly and revealed simultaneously. Each player must be able to pay their chosen card's Wood cost. Return played Contribution cards to their owners after firing; they may be used again in later rounds.

**Base Heat = 2 + sum of final Contribution modifiers, clamped to 0–5.**
Examples:

- Tend + Tend + Tend → Base Heat 2.
- Stoke + Tend + Tend → Base Heat 3.
- Bank + Tend + Tend → Base Heat 1.
- Stoke + Bank + Tend → Base Heat 2; both players still pay their Wood.

**Global Heat = Base Heat + the revealed Fire modifier.**

**Actual Heat = Global Heat + the ceramic's zone modifier + its personal Heat adjustments.** High adds **+1**, Middle **0**, Low **−1**; an Imperial Kiln has no zone modifier. Neither Global Heat nor Actual Heat is capped.

**Heat Difference = the absolute difference between Actual Heat and Preferred Heat.** The same revealed Fire card applies to all ceramics in both kinds of kiln, except a ceramic affected by Second Firing. See Appendix D for the complete ability sequence.

### Quality Table

| Heat Difference | Quality | Order Eligibility |
|:---:|---|---|
| 0 | **Masterpiece** | Meets any minimum Quality. |
| 1 | **Fine** | Meets Fine+ and Standard+. |
| 2 | **Standard** | Meets Standard+ only. |
| 3+ | **Flawed** | Cannot complete Orders or enter the Exhibition. |

After all after-Quality abilities and Ge's Crackle ability have resolved, each player may discard **1 ceramic that is still Flawed from this firing** to gain **2 Coins**.

## Phase 4 — Order Phase

The Order Phase uses **reverse Work-Phase seating order**: the player immediately counter-clockwise from the First Player acts first, then play proceeds counter-clockwise.
On each Order opportunity, a player may complete **at most 1 Order** or pass.
A completed Order may be:

- one of that player's held Orders; or
- one of the **6 face-up Main Orders**.

If a face-up Main Order is completed, remove it, slide later Orders left and refill the rightmost position immediately.
Continue cycling until one full circuit occurs in which **no player completes an Order**.
Passing on one opportunity does not prevent a player from completing an Order on a later circuit if the display changes.

## Phase 5 — Cleanup

1. Return all workers to their owners.
2. Discard held Orders of your choice down to the hand limit of 3.
3. Return discarded Starting Orders to the box; place discarded Main Orders in the Main Order discard pile.
4. If this was Round 5, proceed to final scoring. Otherwise, pass the First Player marker clockwise and advance the Round marker.

# Workshop Development

A player's workshop consists of:
> **1 Starting Tech + 1 Kiln Tradition + up to 2 Advanced Techs**

## Tech Effects and Worker Actions

**A Tech effect is not a worker action.**
If a Tech lets you form, apply a Decoration, glaze or load, that does not count as taking the corresponding worker action and does not trigger effects requiring that worker action unless explicitly stated otherwise.

An effect beginning **“After each [action]”** may be used **once after each matching worker action**, not once per vessel. An effect triggered by forming, decorating or loading a vessel may apply when a Tech performs that operation, subject to its own restrictions.

## Optional Abilities and Costs

All **Starting Tech, Advanced Tech and Kiln Tradition abilities are optional**, unless explicitly stated to be mandatory.

- To use an ability, meet all requirements and pay all required costs in full. Apply valid discounts and waivers before payment. If you cannot pay, you cannot use the ability.
- Declining an optional ability does not use it or spend its cost.
- Once an ability is used, resolve all required instructions and consequences.
- Timing restrictions and usage limits still apply.
- If several players have abilities in the same timing window, resolve them in **First Player order**. Each player chooses the order of their own abilities.

## Starting Techs

Each player chooses exactly 1 Starting Tech during Setup. Starting Techs do not count toward the Advanced-Tech limit and score no VP.

## Advanced Tech Rules

- Exactly 5 Forming, 5 Decoration & Glazing and 5 Firing Advanced Techs exist. Each Advanced Tech is **unique: only one copy** is available.
- A player may own at most 2.
- Advanced Techs modify existing actions, production or firing; they do not create worker positions.
- Follow each tile's timing and usage limit.
- If you control multiple abilities with the same timing, choose their order unless a rule specifies otherwise.
- Each Advanced Tech scores **1 VP at game end**.

# Orders and Imperial Recognition

## Main Order Market

- There is one **48-card Main Order deck** and one **6-card public display**.
- Starting Orders are a separate 8-card deck used only during Setup.
- Whenever a face-up Main Order leaves the display because it is completed or reserved, slide later Orders left and refill the rightmost position immediately.
- Commercial Orders tend to be more flexible and pay more Coins. Crown Orders tend to be more specific, pay fewer Coins and advance Imperial Recognition.

### Reserving Orders

Use the Commission Market to reserve Main Orders.
A reserved Order:

- enters your secret hand;
- is protected from other players;
- counts toward your hand limit during Cleanup;
- can be completed in any later Order Phase;
- scores exactly as printed.

### Completing Orders

During an Order opportunity:

1. choose 1 held Order or 1 face-up Main Order;
2. if held, reveal it;
3. select the required finished, undelivered ceramics;
4. confirm all Shape, Glaze, Decoration, relationship and minimum Quality requirements;
5. place used ceramics beneath the Order;
6. record its VP;
7. gain its Coins;
8. resolve Crowns one at a time;
9. place the completed Order face up in your scoring area;
10. if it came from the public display, update the display immediately.

A ceramic may satisfy only one completed Order.

### Reading Multi-Ceramic Requirements

Requirements are independent unless a specific pairing is explicitly printed.

For example:

> **Bowl + Brush Washer; one White, one Moon White; one Carved, one Painted**

requires exactly one Bowl and one Brush Washer, one White and one Moon White Glaze, and one Carved and one Painted Decoration. Glazes and Decorations may be assigned to the Shapes independently.

- Each required ceramic is distinct. One ceramic cannot fill two positions on an Order.
- **Carved, Impressed or Painted** means any **one** of those Decorations; Plain does not qualify.
- **Same non-Plain Decoration** means all required ceramics share one choice of **Carved, Impressed or Painted**.
- Plain counts for unrestricted **any**, **same** and **different Decoration** requirements. For example, Plain + Painted are different Decorations, and Plain + Plain share the same Decoration.
- Each Crackle ceramic uses its chosen Decoration consistently for **all checks on that Order**. Its Shape, Glaze and actual Quality must still meet the requirements.

### Imperial Recognition Track

Imperial Recognition may be advanced by Crown icons on completed Orders or by the Imperial Court.

| Recognition | Milestone | Reward when first reached |
|:---:|---|---|
| 0 | Local Workshop | — |
| 👑 1 | Imperial Grant | Gain **3 Coins** or **1 Clay + 1 Wood + 1 Coin**. |
| 👑👑 2 | Imperial Gift | Gain your **Imperial Kiln tile**. |
| 👑👑👑 3 | Imperial Priority | Take your Imperial Priority token. |
| 👑👑👑👑 4 | Imperial Audience | Gain **6 VP**. |
| +👑 | Beyond 4 | Each additional Crown scores **1 VP immediately**. |

- Resolve Crowns one at a time.
- The Imperial Grant, Imperial Kiln, Imperial Priority and Imperial Audience are each gained only once.

## Imperial Kiln

When you first reach **Imperial Recognition 2**, take an Imperial Kiln tile.

- The Imperial Kiln can hold **1 ceramic per firing**.
- During a Kiln Yard action, one ceramic loaded by that action may be placed in your empty Imperial Kiln instead of the Shared Kiln.
- A Tech effect may load the Imperial Kiln only if it explicitly says so.
- Its ceramic fires during the normal shared Firing Phase.
- Its ceramic has **Actual Heat = Global Heat** before personal abilities and receives no High/Middle/Low modifier.
- It counts for Contribution participation and firing abilities.
- It does not occupy a Shared Kiln space.
- After firing, move its ceramic to the owner's Finished Ceramics area.

### Imperial Priority

At **Imperial Recognition 3**, take your Imperial Priority token.
**Once per game, during the Work Phase, before or after your worker action:**

1. choose **1 of your workshop ceramics**, with any Decoration including Plain;
2. pay the **1-Coin Glazing cost**;
3. apply any one Glaze;
4. load it into your empty Imperial Kiln.

Return the spent Imperial Priority token to the box. The ceramic remains in your Imperial Kiln for the coming Firing Phase.

Imperial Priority is not a Kiln Yard action and does not trigger effects requiring that action. A ceramic loaded this way cannot receive a Kiln Yard Shifu marker.

## End-game Exhibition

At game end, exhibit any number of your undelivered ceramics of Standard Quality or better.

| Quality | VP each |
|---|:---:|
| Standard | 2 |
| Fine | 3 |
| Masterpiece | 5 |

Then score each diversity bonus once:

- +3 VP for at least **3 different Shapes**;
- +3 VP for at least **3 different Glazes**.

Flawed ceramics cannot be exhibited.

# Kiln Tradition Abilities

Player boards may use abbreviated reminder text. The rules here give the full effects.

## Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection

**Once per round**, when you complete an Order using a **Masterpiece with Celadon Glaze and Plain Decoration**, gain **4 VP**.
The Order itself does not need to require those attributes; the delivered ceramic must actually have them.

## Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage

**Once per round**, when you complete an Order showing at least 1 Crown, gain **2 Coins and 1 VP**.

## Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle from Fire

**Once per round, after resolving your other after-Quality abilities:** choose **1 of your Standard ceramics from this firing**. It develops **Crackle** and becomes **Fine**. Place a Crackle marker on it.

When completing an Order, each of your Crackle ceramics may be treated as having **any one Decoration** for that Order.

- **Crackle is permanent** and is neither a Decoration nor a Glaze. Keep the marker with its ceramic until that ceramic is delivered, discarded or otherwise leaves play.
- The ceramic's **actual Glaze and Decoration do not change**. Crackle does **not** provide a wild Glaze.
- Choose one Decoration independently for each Crackle ceramic used in an Order, and use that choice for every requirement on that Order. This substitution applies only to Order requirements.
- Its actual Quality is **Fine**, including for Exhibition. Use its actual Glaze when checking Exhibition diversity.

## Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production

**Once per round**, during an **Apprentice's Potter's Wheel action**, after you form a **Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer**, pay **1 Clay** to form **1 additional vessel of the same Shape**.

- The additional vessel starts **Plain for free** and does not count against that action's vessel limit.
- It counts as formed by that Potter's Wheel action for **White Slip**, **Drying Frames** and other effects referring to vessels formed by that action.
- This ability does **not** trigger during a Shifu action or from forming through a Tech effect.

## Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation

**Once per round**, after Actual Heat is calculated but before Quality is assigned, pay **1 Wood** to adjust the Actual Heat of **1 of your ceramics by +1 or −1**.

# End of the Game and Scoring

The game ends after Round 5 Cleanup.
Score:

1. all VP recorded during play, including Orders, Kiln Tradition bonuses, Imperial Audience and Crowns beyond Recognition 4;
2. **1 VP for each Advanced Tech** you own;
3. the End-game Exhibition;
4. **1 VP per 3 Coins remaining**, maximum **5 VP**.

The player with the most VP wins.

## Tie Breakers

1. Farther along Imperial Recognition.
2. More Crown icons printed across completed Orders.
3. More Masterpiece ceramics delivered or exhibited.
4. If still tied, share victory.

# General Clarifications

- **Unlimited resources:** Clay, Wood and Coins are unlimited. If supplied components run out, use suitable substitutes or record the additional amounts. Make each substitute's resource type and value clear.
- Gain resources from the general supply and return spent resources to it.
- **Resources are open information.** Players must accurately state their Clay, Wood and Coin totals on request.
- Contribution choices remain secret until revealed.
- **No trading:** Players may not trade resources, Orders, Techs, ceramics or other components.

# Appendix D — Full Firing Sequence

**Before this sequence:** resolve **Glaze Palette** and other end-of-Work-Phase abilities. No such ability may be delayed until after Test Pieces or Contributions.

If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, skip this phase and do not reveal a Fire card. Resolve players' abilities in **First Player order** at each applicable timing window; each player chooses the order of their own effects unless a specific timing rule says otherwise.

1. **Pre-firing abilities.** Resolve Test Pieces and similar effects before Contributions are chosen.
2. **Choose Contributions.** Every participating player secretly chooses **1 Contribution card** and must be able to pay its Wood cost. A player with Fuel Ledger may choose **+2 Stoke** or **−2 Bank**, costing **2 Wood**, instead of a standard Contribution.
3. **Reveal and pay.** Reveal all Contribution cards simultaneously, then pay their Wood costs.
4. **Determine Base Heat.** Start at 2, add all Contribution modifiers, then clamp to **0–5**.
5. **Kiln Yard Shifu adjustment.** Each player whose Shifu is on a ceramic may replace that Shifu with a **+1 or −1 Heat marker** on that ceramic. It must be a ceramic **loaded by that Shifu action**, in either kiln. The adjustment is optional, costs no Wood and affects only that ceramic's Actual Heat. Set removed Shifu aside until Cleanup.
6. **Reveal Fire.** Reveal the top Fire card face up and add its modifier to Base Heat. This is **Global Heat**, which is not capped. Keep this main Fire card face up until the firing finishes.
7. **Calculate Actual Heat.** Shared-Kiln ceramics add their zone modifier; Imperial Kiln ceramics have no zone modifier. Kiln Furniture makes its ceramic's zone modifier **0**. Then add any Shifu Heat marker. Actual Heat is not capped.
8. **Heat-adjustment abilities.** Resolve Jun and any other effects that occur after Actual Heat is calculated and before Quality is assigned.
9. **Assign Quality.** Compare final Actual Heat with Preferred Heat. Use the absolute difference and the Quality table.
10. **After-Quality abilities.** Resolve Protective Saggars, Second Firing and similar effects. If you control several, choose their order. Second Firing replaces only its selected ceramic's result; it does not change the shared Global Heat or other ceramics.
11. **Ge — Crackle from Fire.** After the other after-Quality effects are complete, Ge may choose **1 of its Standard ceramics from this firing**, give it Crackle and change its Quality to **Fine**. Crackle provides a wild **Decoration only** when completing Orders.
12. **Salvage.** Each player may discard **1 ceramic still Flawed from this firing** to gain **2 Coins**.
13. **Finish firing.** Move remaining fired ceramics to their owners' Finished Ceramics areas. Empty all kiln spaces, return Kiln Furniture, remove Shifu Heat markers, set any Shifu still on ceramics aside until Cleanup, discard the main Fire card face up and return Contribution cards to their owners. Crackle markers remain with their ceramics.

# Appendix E — Quick Reference

## Round Sequence

1. **Start:** rotate and refill Main Orders; ready abilities.
2. **Work:** First Player clockwise; place all **4 workers**, with no passing. Then resolve **Glaze Palette** and other end-of-Work-Phase abilities.
3. **Fire:** Contributions → Base Heat → optional Shifu Heat marker → Fire → Actual Heat → abilities → Quality → after-Quality effects → Ge Crackle → salvage.
4. **Orders:** reverse Work order; one Order per opportunity; repeat until a full circuit completes no Orders.
5. **Cleanup:** return workers; trim Orders to 3; after Round 5 score the game, otherwise pass First Player and advance the round.
