# Kiln Opening V1.2.6 — player changes

V1.2.6 makes three focused rules updates. All other V1.2.5 rules, values, decks, scoring, timing and action capacities remain unchanged.

## Main Order display

The five face-up Main Orders form a queue from oldest on the left to newest on the right. Whenever a face-up Order is reserved or completed, remove it, slide every later Order left, and add the replacement at the right. Blind top-deck reservations and privately viewed Colour Samples reservations do not move the display.

Resolve a Shifu's reservations one at a time, including the display movement and resource gain after the first, before choosing the second. At the start of Rounds 2–5, discard the two leftmost Orders, retain the other three in order, and add two new Orders at the right.

## Kiln Yard Shifu

When you take a Kiln Yard action with your Shifu, load up to two ceramics as before. If you then have at least one of your ceramics in the Shared Kiln, place your Shifu on exactly one of them.

After Contributions are revealed and Base Heat is determined—but before the Fire card is revealed—you may move only that marked ceramic to an empty active space in a neighbouring heat zone. Resolve multiple Shifu in First Player order. Kiln Furniture moves with the ceramic. A Shifu cannot mark or move a ceramic in an Imperial Kiln.

If you load only into your Imperial Kiln and have no ceramic in the Shared Kiln, you do not mark a ceramic and receive no Shifu reposition for that firing.

## Ding Kiln — Moulded Production

Ding's once-per-round additional Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer now costs exactly 1 Clay. It remains separate from the vessel or vessels formed by the worker effect:

- Apprentice forms one Bowl and Ding forms one Bowl: 2 Clay total.
- Shifu forms two 1-Clay vessels without Ding: 1 Clay total after the Shifu discount.
- Shifu forms one Bowl and Ding forms one Bowl: 2 Clay total.
- Shifu forms two 1-Clay vessels and Ding forms one matching vessel: 2 Clay total.

The Ding vessel does not count toward the worker effect's vessel limit or toward whether the Shifu discount applies. It may still trigger Standardised Moulds normally.

# 《开窑》V1.2.6 — 玩家改动说明

V1.2.6 仅调整以下三项规则。其余 V1.2.5 规则、数值、牌组、计分、时机与行动容量均保持不变。

## 主委托展示

5 张公开主委托组成一条队列：最左侧最旧，最右侧最新。每当承接或完成 1 张公开委托时，将其移走，其右侧所有委托向左移动，再将补充的委托放入最右侧。从牌库盲抽或承接通过色样簿私下查看的委托不会移动展示。

师傅的多次承接须逐张结算：完成第 1 次承接的展示移动与资源收益后，再决定第 2 次。第 2–5 轮开始时，弃掉最左侧 2 张，依原顺序保留其余 3 张，再将 2 张新委托加入右端。

## 窑坊师傅

用师傅执行窑坊行动时，仍然可以装窑至多 2 件陶瓷。装窑后，若你的共窑中至少有 1 件己方陶瓷，必须将师傅放在其中恰好 1 件上。

控火牌揭示并确定基础火候后、火牌揭示前，你只能将师傅所在的那件陶瓷移动至相邻火候区中的一个空置有效窑位。若有多位师傅，按起始玩家顺序结算。支烧窑具随陶瓷移动。师傅不能放在御窑陶瓷上，也不能将陶瓷移入或移出御窑。

若本次只将陶瓷装入御窑，且你的共窑中没有己方陶瓷，则不放置师傅，也不能进行此次调位。

## 定窑 — 范制成器

定窑每轮一次额外成型的碗、盘或笔洗现在须额外支付 1 泥。该器物与工人效果所成型的器物分开计算：

- 学徒成型 1 个碗，定窑再成型 1 个碗：共支付 2 泥。
- 师傅成型 2 件费用各为 1 泥的器物，不使用定窑：师傅折扣后共支付 1 泥。
- 师傅成型 1 个碗，定窑再成型 1 个碗：共支付 2 泥。
- 师傅成型 2 件费用各为 1 泥的器物，定窑再成型 1 件相同器物：共支付 2 泥。

定窑额外器物不计入工人效果的器物数量，也不用于判断是否获得师傅折扣；它仍可正常触发定式陶范。
