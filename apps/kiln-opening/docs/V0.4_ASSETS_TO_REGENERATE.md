# V1.2.5 assets to regenerate (legacy filename)

The filename remains unchanged to preserve existing links. Rebuild these assets from the supplied V1.2.5 sources and current structured data rather than copying older print prototypes.

## 1. Starting and Main Order cards

Generate all 64 fronts from `data/orders.json`: 16 separate Starting Orders and 48 unified Main Orders. Main Orders visibly print Coin, VP and Crown rewards. Use the exact English and Simplified Chinese requirements associated with each stable ID and distinct Starting/Main backs.

## 2. Central Action Board and Shared Kiln

Generate Materials Yard, Potter's Wheel, Glaze & Decoration, Kiln Yard, Commission Market, Guild & Academy and Labour from `data/action_locations.json`. Materials, Wheel, Glaze, Commission and Guild have one global 2/3/4 worker capacity at 2/3/4 players; Kiln Yard and Labour are uncapped. Any worker may take any unoccupied printed space regardless of another worker at that location, and a Shifu may overfill a full location. Include the Round and 0–4 Imperial Recognition tracks, but do not render Recognition as an action location.

The Shared Kiln has 3 High (+1), 2 Middle (0) and 2 Low (−1) spaces. Use all 7 at 4 players, cover 1 High at 3 players, and cover 1 High plus 1 Middle at 2 players.

Do not show Refining House, Refined Clay, separate Market/Imperial displays, Office, Court Patronage, private Forming/Glazing worker spaces, worker or workshop-space unlocks, the Imperial Seal, numeric Wood bids, Kiln Yard Wood income or an Imperial heat-adjustment token.

## 3. Five Kiln player boards

Generate from `data/kilns.json`. Every board shows one Starting Tech, up to two Advanced Techs, an empty Imperial Kiln area that receives its one-ceramic tile at Recognition 2, and Imperial Priority token state. Do not add private Potter's Wheel or Glaze & Decoration worker stations; both are shared Central Action Board locations and no Tech unlocks a worker position.

## 4. Starting and Advanced Tech sets

Generate four Starting Tech designs with four copies each, plus all 15 Advanced Tech tiles from `data/techniques.json`, with matching English and Simplified Chinese names, costs, timings and ability text. A player may own at most two Advanced Techs.

## 5. Contribution and Fire cards

Generate four player sets of Bank the Fire, Tend the Fire and Stoke the Fire: 12 Contribution cards total. Generate the separate 12-card Fire deck from `data/firing.json` with the distribution 1/3/4/3/1 at −2/−1/0/+1/+2.
