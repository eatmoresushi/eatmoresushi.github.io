## Authority and provenance

On 2026-10-18: the keyword Select N, Fuel Ledger's Contribution cards and the end of Tech VP, in all three files ("Owner revision 3", rulebook changes 25–27). ### Owner revision 2, 2026-10-17: Starting Orders, Main Order values, looked cards to the deck bottom

The owner added change-log items 23 and 24 (L33–34, Chinese lines 35–36) and applied them in all three files:

- **Change 23, the Starting Order deck: 16 → 8 cards** (L90, L109–110, L391, A0 at L523–536). The new S01–S08 are four Glaze cards (White, Celadon, Grey-Green 3 VP + 4 Coins; Moon White 4 + 4), S05 "Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer" 3 + 4, S06 "Vase or Censer" 3 + 5, S07 "Carved, Impressed or Painted" 3 + 3 and S08 "any" 2 + 4. Setup still deals 2 Starting Orders and 1 Main Order to each player; a 4-player game deals all 8 and none go to the box (L110, L525). The IDs S01–S08 now name the new cards; S09–S16 are retired.
- **Change 24, the Main Order re-pass** (Appendix A): nineteen cards change value. O01–O03 4 VP; O04 7 + 3; O05 6 + 3; O14 5 + 3; O15 5 + 4; O19 and O20 6 VP; O22 9; O26 11; O27 12; O28 11; O30 13; O32 16; O33 and O34 15 + 5; O38 12; O39 13.
- **Change 24, the deck flow** (L34, L197, L201–202, L401, L622; component text line 42): looked-at Orders not reserved go to the **bottom of the Main Order deck**, not the discard pile. At revision 2 this covered the Commission Market Apprentice's 2 and Shifu's 3 cards, Colour Samples' when-acquired 3 and the other card of its swap's 2 (revision 3 made every one of these a Select, below). It supersedes owner ruling R14. Several cards put there together go in random order (owner ruling R19, 2026-10-18). The held Order a swap discards still goes face down to the discard (a Starting Order to the box, L646), and so do the Orders discarded at round start (L136) and at Cleanup (L309). **Reversed by owner ruling R25 (2026-10-23):** looked cards not reserved are discarded again (owner revision 4 subsection below).

### Owner revision 3, 2026-10-18: Select N, Fuel Ledger as cards, no Tech VP

The owner added change-log items 25–27 (L35–37, Chinese lines 37–39) and applied them in all three files:

- **Change 25, the keyword Select N** (L35, glossary L64, L197, L201–202, L401, L622, L646, L649, L710, L715–716; component text, keyword line 3). "Look at the top N cards of the Main Order deck. Reserve 1 of them, or 1 face-up Main Order from the display; put the rest of the N on the bottom of the deck" (L64). The Commission Market Apprentice Selects 2; the Shifu (and Senior Apprentice, L649) Selects 3, reserving up to 2 in any mix of the 3 cards and the display (L202). Colour Samples Selects 3 when acquired, and its once-a-round swap is "discard 1 held Order to Select 3" (L622). Change 22 (L32) was edited in place to match: the swap looks at "the top 3", not "the top 2".
- **Change 26, Fuel Ledger as cards** (L36, L93, L111, L246, L266, L508, L634, L668–669, L734; component text, the Fuel Ledger tile and a new section for its 2 cards). The secret 1-Wood commitment is gone. The tile brings 2 Contribution cards, **Fuel Ledger Bank** (1 Wood, −2) and **Fuel Ledger Stoke** (1 Wood, +2), kept beside the Firing deck at setup and taken by whoever acquires Fuel Ledger (L111, L634). Its owner chooses 1 of their 5 cards and plays exactly 1 (L266, L634, L668); plain Bank and Stoke still cost them no Wood. The game has 14 Contribution cards with one back (L93). Wood is paid only on reveal, so none is set aside beforehand (L508).
- **Change 27, Tech VP removed** (L37, L496, L605; revision-2 lines 378 and 746 deleted, and the Tech VP clause gone from L717). Gaining an Advanced Tech scores nothing, during play or at game end. It supersedes owner amendment A1. Connoisseur's Cabinet keeps its end-game VP (L616); printed costs are unchanged.

- L415 "Choose the required finished, undelivered ceramics" (was "Select", which is now a keyword).
- The English restores the note "Yours, from the doc comments: …" (L39) that revision 2 had replaced; the Chinese edition never dropped it (Chinese line 41).
- The component text shortens Second Firing's tile text in both languages (component text lines 89 and 91), to "1 of your Flawed or Standard ceramics from this firing → reveal 2 Fire cards and recalculate its Quality with 1 of them, even if worse" / 己方本窑1件瑕品或良品→另翻2张窑火牌，以其中1张重算其品质，即使更差也算. It drops "choose", "instead of the first" and "(Jun's earlier adjustment is lost)", and the Chinese drops 可选. The full rule (L636) is unchanged, so the effect is the same; the component text's header still makes every ability optional unless it says otherwise (component text lines 3 and 5). The data's `shortText` and `shortTextZh` for T14 follow the new lines.

### Owner revision 4, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23: no Heat marker, Ge, quick wins, the starting Main Order, the Magistrate's Hall Shifu, kiln fuel; owner ruling R25

Line numbers in this subsection are revision-4 lines: lines of the English source as it stands now, after change 35 and R25's edit (737 lines). The Chinese edition is the English line plus 2 from English line 3 on (739 lines). The owner added change-log items 28–34 on 2026-10-22 (L38–44, Chinese lines 40–46) and item 35 on 2026-10-23 (L45, Chinese line 47), and applied them in all three files:

- **Changes 28 and 31, no player Heat marker** (L38, L41). Change 28 first swapped the Heat-marker step and Test Pieces; change 31 then removed the player Heat marker altogether: no face-down token and no marker step, and the Glaze & Load Shifu "simply loads up to 2" (L41, L194). Deleted: the Heat marker glossary row, the Heat markers component row, the Shifu's marker placement, the bullet "The Heat marker is resolved in the Firing Phase", the Heat Markers section, the Heat markers in the negotiation sentence (L501), the Firing table's Test Pieces and Heat markers rows (L251–256), Appendix D's marker step and the quick-reference marker bullet. What change 28 leaves is Test Pieces "after Base Heat is determined and before the Fire card is revealed" (L626, L636; component text line 84; the timing vocabulary's "end of 1", L670). **Kiln Master** (L628, L635; component text line 94): "Once per round, when you load a ceramic, you may place a +1 or −1 marker on it. It adds its value to that ceramic's Actual Heat." The marker is chosen at loading, face up and open information, like the Imperial modifier (L635). It counts in Actual Heat in both kilns (L73, L78, L255, L292, L661, L719); Jun applies on top of it (L478, L635); it stays through a Second Firing recalculation (L627, L635) and returns when the firing finishes (L664). Appendix D is now 6 steps (L659–664): Contributions (Base Heat) → Fire → Actual Heat (Jun "applies now") → Quality → After-Quality abilities → Finish firing.
- **Change 29, Ge** (L39, L463, L465, L479–480, L641, L728; component text lines 128 and 130). Once per round, after Quality, 1 of your **Fine or Standard** ceramics from this firing develops Crackle "and is Fine (a Standard one becomes Fine)" (L463). "Each Crackle ceramic you deliver to complete an Order scores 1 VP" (L465), which triggers while completing an Order in the Order Phase (L480). "A Crackle ceramic is never a Masterpiece" is dropped as redundant (L39). Saggars' Standard → Fine step and Crackle "may both apply to the same ceramic, since Crackle does not change a Fine ceramic's Quality" (L641).
- **Change 30, quick wins** (L40). Fuel Ledger costs **4** (L625). Forming costs the Shape's Clay plus **2 Coins** with any Decoration; the Decoration table is gone (L183–186, L336, L691). The Shifu's −1 Coin when forming 2, Decorator's Kit's −1 Coin for a Carved, Impressed or Painted vessel and Ding's 1-Clay-1-Coin vessel are unchanged, now as reductions of a vessel's cost (L184, L469, L603, L639); Prepared Clay pays its Clay + 1 Clay and 2 Coins (L605). Form & Decorate legality counts reductions (L186). An unused Jun adjustment may apply to a Second Firing recalculation (L478, L627; this was owner ruling R7). **A Flawed ceramic may be sold at any time for 2 Coins** (L282, L284, L729); the firing-step salvage of 1 still-Flawed ceramic is gone. The Kiln Die is the day's weather (L140; flavour). Change 30 also introduced a seat Coin for every player after the first; change 35 withdrew it before any revision-4 rule text kept it: Setup step 12 (L125) gives every player the same resources.
- **Change 32, the starting Main Order is chosen** (L42, L115–116, L122, L516). Only the 2 Starting Orders are dealt (L115), and the 6 face-up Main Orders are revealed without the former reshuffle of the Main deck (L116; nothing Main is dealt). In reverse turn order each player chooses a Kiln Player Board and takes 1 face-up Main Order from the display into their hand; the display is refilled to 6 after each take, and everyone has seen which Main Order each player took (L122).
- **Change 33, the Magistrate's Hall Shifu** (L43, L241, L245, L392, L696). The Shifu pays **4 Coins** (was 3), advances 1 step, "then reserve[s] 1 face-up Crown Order from the display" (L241); with no Crown Order showing, Recognition still advances and nothing is reserved; the reserved Order's position stays empty until the end of the turn, then the display is refilled as at the Commission Market (L245).
- **Changes 34 and 35, the kiln burns fuel** (L44–45, L92, L176–177, L197–198, L291, L316, L690, L692, L706). "A Glaze & Load action that loads into the Shared Kiln costs **1 Wood**, whether it loads 1 vessel or 2. You pay **no Wood** if the action loads only into your Imperial Kiln" (L197). A Glaze & Load placement is legal only with a Shaped vessel, an empty active Shared Kiln space or your own empty Imperial Kiln, "and 1 Wood if you load into the Shared Kiln" (L198). Loading into the Imperial Kiln costs no Wood (L291, L706). Materials Yard: Apprentice **4**, Shifu **8** (L176–177, L690). Starting resources **2 Clay, 3 Wood and 3 Coins** (L125). Change 34 as first exported (2026-10-22) charged each ceramic loaded into the Shared Kiln, the Shifu paying for at most 1 of its 2; change 35 (2026-10-23) restated the fuel per action, "identical Wood on every line", and withdrew the seat Coin (L45).

The other edits are wording: Decorator's Kit (L603) and the Discounts clarification (L639) speak of a vessel's cost instead of a Decoration's; Ding (L469) forms its extra vessel "with any Decoration"; the Reserving Orders paragraph names the Magistrate's Hall Shifu (L392) and the display paragraph no longer names the Commission Market (L386); the Components row for Wood says what it is burnt for (L92); production stage 3 adds "(1 Wood)" (L316); Imperial Gift in the quick reference adds "pay no Wood when loading into it" (L706); the quick reference adds the heat formulas (L719); Appendix A0 says each player chooses 1 face-up Main Order (L516).

In Round 5 it is never fired and scores nothing (only Finished ceramics are exhibited): legal but pointless; the computer players never do it. Confirmation asked (open question 4).
- **Completion effects across windows** (I5; change 36; L176, L474, L478, L484, L499, L637, L648): Ru, Guan, Tribute Ware and Canal Barge are once per round, whichever window the completion is in: one use shared by the round's Work turns and its Order Phase. Ru, Guan and Tribute Ware stay optional (R4): a decline keeps the use for a later completion that round, in any window. Canal Barge stays automatic on the round's first completion (INT Canal Barge). Crackle VP has no limit. Rewards resolve at once, so a completion before the worker action may pay for it (Coins), change its legality (a Crown reaching Recognition 3 closes the Magistrate's Hall) or open a destination (the Imperial Kiln at Recognition 2) (L176).
- **Ge Crackle (revision 5)** (I5; change 39; L482, L484, L660, L747): once per round, after Quality, Ge targets any 1 of the owner's ceramics from this firing, in the Shared Kiln or the Imperial Kiln, whatever its Quality. A Standard one becomes Fine; a Masterpiece, Fine or Flawed one keeps its Quality (a Flawed one gains nothing, as change 39 says). It carries a Crackle marker until delivered, exhibited or (a Flawed one) sold. A Crackle ceramic is therefore no longer always Fine: a crackled Masterpiece fills Masterpiece requirements, exhibits at 5 VP, counts for Connoisseur's Cabinet and for tie-breaker 3, and scores its Crackle VP when delivered. Second Firing may take a crackled Flawed ceramic (it is Flawed; the owner orders their after-Quality abilities, L682); the recalculation replaces the Quality and the marker stays; Ge's Standard → Fine applies only when Crackle develops, never again. With Protective Saggars, Saggars' Standard → Fine and then Crackle may apply to one ceramic (L660); Crackle first on a Standard leaves it Fine, out of Saggars' reach. Crackle stays optional (R4).
- **Decorator's Kit (revision 5)** (change 37; L354, L622, L658): once per round, 1 Coin off any vessel you form, any Decoration; it may be the vessel the Shifu's discount reduces (that vessel's 2 Coins become 0). It may reduce Prepared Clay's 2 Coins too (L624, "a vessel you form"), but both are Form & Decorate Techs, so no player owns both. Form & Decorate legality counts it on any vessel (L204).
- **Ding and the discounts (revision 5)** (I4; change 40; L202, L488, L658): Ding's extra vessel costs 1 Clay + 2 Coins with any Decoration, is formed after a Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer of the Form & Decorate action, does not count against the action's vessel limit and counts as formed by the action for effects that refer to its vessels. Either reduction (the Shifu's discount, Decorator's Kit) could in principle go on Ding's vessel, but each is once per action or round and the vessel that triggers Ding can always carry it (change 40), so totals and affordability are the same wherever it goes; the online game applies the reductions to the action's ordinary vessels. Whether Ding's vessel counts toward the Shifu's "If you form 2" is not printed: provisional default P6 (it does not).
- **Kiln Master's one marker** (R22 as extended; L105, L647, L654, L683): the tile has one marker, returned when that ceramic's firing finishes. Placed on an Imperial Priority load in the Order Phase, it stays on that ceramic until the next round's firing finishes, so Kiln Master cannot place it again before then, although its once-per-round use is readied at the next Start of Round: provisional default P7.

### Provisional defaults (edge cases the text leaves open; owner may overrule)

P1–P3 come from the V1.6.1 engine design (ENGINE_V1.6.1_DESIGN.md §11). Each can change without changing the engine contract. P4, which the V1.6.2 delta added, was confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-17 and is now owner ruling R15. P5 was recorded on 2026-10-18 for owner revision 2; the owner overruled it the same day with owner ruling R19 (random order), which R25 has since made obsolete. Revisions 3 and 4 add none (revision 4's five questions were answered as R20–R24). Revision 5 adds P6 and P7 (2026-10-30), each asked as an open question below.

- **P1 — Reworking Table with Imperial Priority.** Allowed. It uses Reworking Table's once-per-round allowance. The tile triggers "when you glaze and load a Shaped vessel" and does not require the Glaze & Load action. Still valid (L613, L298, L642); since revision 5 also for Imperial Priority in the Order Phase, with that round's allowance (rev. 5 L623, L652).
- **P2 — More than two empty display positions.** Possible only after the Main deck and discard both ran out (B1). Every refill compacts the display and fills all empty positions as cards allow, aiming at 6. Still valid (L138, L197).
- **P3 — Reworking into Inverted Firing eligibility.** Glaze & Load placement legality (R5c) looks only at each vessel's current Shape. During a legal action, the Shape change applies before the Inverted Firing Shape check (line 613, "first"). Still valid (L613, L625); since revision 4 a Shared Kiln destination also needs 1 Wood (R5).
- **P5 — The order of several looked cards put on the bottom** (change 24; L197, L201–202, L622). **Replaced** by owner ruling R19 (2026-10-18): several cards go there in random order. The provisional default was that they keep the order they were drawn in, the first drawn ending highest and the last drawn the new bottom card, with no extra decision for the player. Kept here as a record; no item or code applies it. Moot since owner ruling R25 (2026-10-23): looked cards not reserved are discarded, not put under the deck.
- **P6 — Ding's vessel and the Shifu's "If you form 2"** (revision 5; rev. 5 L202, L488, L658; I4). Ding's extra vessel does not count toward the Shifu's "If you form 2, one of them costs 1 Coin less": a Shifu who forms 1 vessel and Ding's gets no Shifu discount; a Shifu who forms 2 and Ding's gets one. Reason: Ding's vessel "does not count against the action's vessel limit" (L488), the discount speaks of the vessels the Shifu forms by the action's own effect (L202), and change 40 (L50) says dropping the old clause ("is not one of the 2 vessels for the Shifu's discount") changed nothing. The reductions are applied to the action's ordinary vessels (I4).
- **P7 — Kiln Master's one marker while a carried Imperial ceramic waits** (revision 5; rev. 5 L105, L647, L654, L683; R22). A Kiln Master marker placed on an Imperial Priority load in the Order Phase stays on that ceramic until the next round's firing finishes; until then Kiln Master cannot place its marker again, although its once-per-round use is readied at Start of Round. Reason: the tile comes with its own marker (L105), and the marker returns when the firing finishes (L683). The online game refuses such a placement (`TECH_MARKER_IN_KILN`) and offers none.

### Open questions for the owner

Five questions from owner revision 5 (2026-10-30) are open. The online game applies the default named in each until the owner answers:

1. **Ding and the Shifu's discount.** Does Ding's extra vessel make a Shifu who forms 1 vessel eligible for "If you form 2, one of them costs 1 Coin less" (rev. 5 L202, L488)? Default: no (P6).
2. **Kiln Master's marker on a carried Imperial ceramic.** A marker placed on an Order Phase Imperial Priority load stays on the ceramic until the next round's firing finishes. Is Kiln Master unusable until then, since the tile has one marker (rev. 5 L105, L683)? Default: yes (P7).
3. **R20's after-Quality and Cleanup windows.** R20 named the owner's own after-Quality turn and pending Cleanup trim as sale windows. Change 36 prints the windows (rev. 5 L170, L176): your turn before or after your worker action, and the Order Phase; never during the firing. Please confirm that a Flawed ceramic can no longer be sold in the after-Quality step (including one of this firing) or during the Cleanup trim. Default: it cannot (I1).
4. **Imperial Priority in the Order Phase.** As a free action (rev. 5 L174, L314) Imperial Priority can be spent in the Order Phase, where its ceramic waits in the Imperial Kiln for the next round's firing (and in Round 5 is never fired). The glossary row (L82), the Recognition table (L448) and the quick reference (L726) still say "before or after your worker action". Is the Order Phase use intended, and should those three lines name the free action? Default: legal in the Order Phase (I3).
5. **Delivering an Order the turn it is reserved.** L417 says a held Order "can be completed as a free action on any later turn of yours or after any later firing", while change 36 (L46) gives "reserve and deliver in one turn" as the reason for mid-turn completion, and the Free Actions section (L170, L173) excludes no Order reserved that turn. May an Order reserved by the worker action (or taken by a Colour Samples swap) be completed in the same turn, after that action or swap? Default: yes (INT Same-turn delivery). If not, L417 is right as printed, and the online game would refuse such a completion until the player's next turn or the Order Phase.
