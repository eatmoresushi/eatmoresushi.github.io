## Authority

The adopted copy includes the owner’s later five-card-market amendment.

## Owner-approved rulings and amendment

1. **Five-card Main Order market (2026-09-02).** Reveal 5 Main Orders. At the start of Rounds 2–5, discard the 3 leftmost, slide the remaining 2 left, and refill to 5.
2. **Recognition 4 — Imperial Priority.** Gain an Imperial Priority token. Once per game, during a **Kiln Yard action**, load **1 additional ceramic** into your empty Imperial Kiln. This replaces the obsolete Recognition-4 heat adjustment still present in §8 and Appendix D of the supplied source. It does not adjust heat.
3. **Second Firing.** Immediately reveal one extra Fire card for the selected Flawed or Standard ceramic. Recalculate it with the same Base Heat and position; its new Quality replaces the first result. Other ceramics are unaffected.
4. **Guan Decoration waiver.** The chosen ceramic ignores direct and relational Decoration requirements. Relationship checks continue among the other submitted ceramics; Shape, Glaze, Quality and ceramic count still apply.

Recognition 3 is titled **Imperial Gift**. Recognition 4 is titled **Imperial Priority**.

## Fuel Ledger presentation

An eligible owner sees five secret contribution outcomes: Bank −1/1 Wood, Tend 0/0 Wood, Stoke +1/1 Wood, Fuel Ledger Bank −2/2 Wood, and Fuel Ledger Stoke +2/2 Wood. The base card and extra-Wood commitment stay private until all contributors reveal together.

## Source inconsistencies found

- The supplied source's §8 Recognition-4 post-Fire ±1 token contradicted §9, the Quick Reference and the owner’s ruling; the adopted ruleset now consistently uses Imperial Priority's extra Kiln Yard load.
- §6’s older “return to Glazed for later” description conflicts with Appendix B’s immediate Second Firing; the owner selected immediate resolution.
- “Imperial Kiln Token” has been corrected to “Imperial Priority Token.”
- Appendix E’s Recognition-3 shorthand now uses the approved title “Imperial Gift.”
- Appendix D originally placed Workshop Seconds after ceramics had already left the kiln. The adopted sequence now resolves Workshop Seconds before finishing the firing.
- The contents list an Appendix F, but the supplied file ends at Appendix E.

Older V1.1.6 documents, experiments, assets and saved games cannot override V1.2.2.
