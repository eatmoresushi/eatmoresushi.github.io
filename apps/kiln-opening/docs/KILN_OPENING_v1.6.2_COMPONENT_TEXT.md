# KILN OPENING 开窑 v1.6.2 — Component Text / 组件简文

Short text for the Kiln Player Boards, the 18 Advanced Tech tiles and the 2 Fuel Ledger Contribution cards, in English and Chinese. Format: **timing:** cost → effect. The full rules are in the v1.6.2 Player Rulebook; every ability is optional unless it says otherwise. Keyword **Select N:** look at the top N cards of the Main Order deck, reserve 1 of them or 1 face-up Main Order from the display; the rest go to the bottom of the deck.

窑口板、18张技艺牌与2张柴簿控火牌上的简文，中英对照。格式：**时机：**费用→效果。完整规则见v1.6.2玩家规则书；除注明“须”者外，各项能力均可选用。关键词**精选N：**查看主委托牌堆顶N张，承接其中1张或委托榜上1张，其余放到牌堆底。

# Form & Decorate Techs / 坯作技艺

### Large Throwing Wheel / 大陶车
**Once per round, during a Form & Decorate action forming at least 1 Vase or Censer:** reduce that action's total Clay cost by 2, minimum 0.

**每轮一次，制坯饰纹行动中制有至少1件瓶或香炉时：**该行动的泥总费−2，最低为0。

### Decorator's Kit / 饰坯匣
**Once per round:** a vessel you form with a Carved, Impressed or Painted Decoration costs 1 Coin less. Stacks with the Shifu discount.

**每轮一次：**你制的1件刻花、印花或画花的坯少付1铜钱。可与师傅的减免叠加。

### Reworking Table / 改坯案
**Once per round, when you glaze and load a Shaped vessel:** first change its Shape to any other Shape. Pay no Clay; its Decoration stays.

**每轮一次，为1件坯施釉装窑时：**可先将其器型改为任意其他器型。不付泥，纹饰不变。

### Prepared Clay / 练泥
**Once per round, after gaining resources at the Materials Yard:** pay a vessel's Clay cost + 1 Clay and 2 Coins → form 1 vessel, any Decoration.

**每轮一次，泥柴场行动得到资源后：**可付器型泥费+1泥及2铜钱→制1件坯，纹饰任选。

### Senior Apprentice / 高徒
**Once per round:** 1 Apprentice you place at the Materials Yard or Commission Market uses the Shifu effect instead.

**每轮一次：**你派往泥柴场或瓷牙行的1名学徒可改按师傅效果结算。

### Connoisseur's Cabinet / 清玩斋
**Game end:** each Fine or Masterpiece ceramic in your Exhibition scores +1 VP, maximum +5 VP.

**终局时：**你陈列中的每件上品或臻品+1分，至多+5分。

# Glazing & Loading Techs / 釉作技艺

### Colour Samples / 色样簿
**When acquired:** Select 3. **Ongoing:** your Order hand limit is 5. **Once per round, before or after one of your worker actions:** discard 1 held Order → Select 3.

**习得时：**精选3。**持续：**你的委托上限为5张。**每轮一次，你的一次工人行动之前或之后：**可弃1张在手委托→精选3。

### Kiln Tending / 看火
**Once per round, after a Glaze & Load action that loaded at least 1 ceramic:** gain 1 Clay + 1 Wood.

**每轮一次，至少装窑1件的施釉装窑行动之后：**得1泥1柴。

### Kiln Furniture / 支烧窑具
**Once per round, when you load 1 ceramic into the Shared Kiln:** place this tile beneath it → choose its zone modifier for this firing: −1, 0 or +1.

**每轮一次，将1件器物装入共窑时：**可将本牌垫于其下→本次烧成中，该器物的温区修正由你定为−1、0或+1。

### Inverted Firing / 覆烧
**Once per round, when you load a Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer into the Shared Kiln:** you may place it in a space holding exactly 1 of your ceramics. Both use that space's modifier.

**每轮一次，将1件碗、盘或笔洗装入共窑时：**可放入恰有1件己方器物的启用窑位；两件同用该窑位的修正。

### Setter's Mark / 窑位签
**Once per round, at the start of the Work Phase:** place this tile on an empty active Shared Kiln space → only you may load there this round.

**每轮一次，派工阶段开始时：**可将本牌置于共窑1个空的启用窑位→本轮只有你能在此装窑。

### Canal Barge / 漕船
**Once per round, when you complete an Order:** gain 2 Coins.

**每轮一次，完成委托时：**得2铜钱。

# Firing Techs / 火作技艺

### Protective Saggars / 匣钵护烧
**Always:** your ceramics are never Flawed; a Heat Difference of 3 or more counts as Standard. **Once per round, after Quality is assigned:** 1 of your Standard ceramics from this firing → Fine.

**持续：**你的器物永不为瑕品，火候差3或以上算作良品。**每轮一次，定品后：**己方本窑1件良品→上品。

### Fuel Ledger / 柴簿
**Always:** your Bank and Stoke cost no Wood. **When acquired:** Gain the Fuel Ledger Bank and Fuel Ledger Stoke Contribution cards (pay 1 Wood → −2/+2).

**持续：**你的压火与添柴不付柴。**习得时：**获取柴簿压火与柴簿添柴两张控火牌（付1柴→−2或+2）。

### Test Pieces / 火照
**Once per round, after Base Heat, before the Fire card, if you have a ceramic in this firing:** pay 1 Wood → privately look at the top Fire card; you may put it on the bottom of the deck.

**每轮一次，基础火候定后、翻窑火前，本窑有己方器物时：**可付1柴→私看窑火牌堆顶1张，可将其置于牌堆底。

### Second Firing / 复烧
**Once per round, after Quality is assigned:** 1 of your Flawed or Standard ceramics from this firing → reveal 2 Fire cards and recalculate its Quality with 1 of them, even if worse.

**每轮一次，定品后：**己方本窑1件瑕品或良品→另翻2张窑火牌，以其中1张重算其品质，即使更差也算。

### Kiln Master / 窑工
**Once per round, when you load a ceramic:** you may place a +1 or −1 marker on it. It adds its value to that ceramic's Actual Heat.

**每轮一次，装窑1件器物时：**可在它上面放一个+1或−1标记。其数值计入该器物的实际火候。

### Tribute Ware / 贡瓷
**Once per round, when you complete a Crown Order:** gain 1 additional Crown.

**每轮一次，完成御令委托时：**额外得1个御令。

# Fuel Ledger Contribution Cards / 柴簿控火牌

### Fuel Ledger Bank / 柴簿压火
**1 Wood:** −2.

**1柴：**−2。

### Fuel Ledger Stoke / 柴簿添柴
**1 Wood:** +2.

**1柴：**+2。

# Kiln Player Boards / 窑口板

### Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection / 天青无瑕
**Once per round, when you complete an Order using a Plain Celadon Masterpiece:** gain 4 VP.

**每轮一次，以素面青釉臻品完成委托时：**得4分。

### Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage / 内府恩眷
**Once per round, when you complete a Crown Order:** gain 2 Coins + 1 VP.

**每轮一次，完成御令委托时：**得2铜钱和1分。

### Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle Mastery / 金丝铁线
**Once per round, after Quality is assigned:** 1 of your Fine or Standard ceramics from this firing → Crackle; it is Fine. **Orders:** each Crackle ceramic may count as any 1 Decoration and scores 1 VP when delivered.

**每轮一次，定品后：**可令己方本窑1件上品或良品开片→品质为上品。**完成委托时：**每件开片器可作任意1种纹饰，交付时得1分。

### Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production / 范制成器
**Once per round, during a Form & Decorate action:** after forming a Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer, pay 1 Clay + 1 Coin → form 1 extra vessel of the same Shape with any Decoration (no discounts). It counts as formed by that action.

**每轮一次，制坯饰纹行动中制成碗、盘或笔洗后：**可付1泥1铜钱→多制1件同器型的坯，纹饰任选，不享减免。算作该行动所制。

### Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation / 窑变天成
**Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated, before Quality:** pay 1 Wood → adjust 1 of your ceramics' Actual Heat by +1 or −1.

**每轮一次，实际火候算出后、定品前：**可付1柴→己方1件器物的实际火候+1或−1。
