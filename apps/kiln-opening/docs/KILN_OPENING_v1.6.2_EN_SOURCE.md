# KILN OPENING 开窑 v1.6.2 — Player Rulebook (Draft)

Oct 11, 2026 · @Allen

**2–4 players | 90–120 minutes | Ages 12+** *Build the workshop. Share the kiln. Master the craft.* A medium-weight worker-placement game about Song Dynasty ceramic workshops.

## Changes from v1.2.7

Version v1.6.2 (the working name v1.3 was already used by an earlier draft). This draft applies every decided change and removes all text that referred to the Potter's Wheel, Glaze & Decoration, Kiln Yard, Starting Techs, the Shifu overfill rule, the Kiln Yard reposition, the Glazed/unloaded state and the old Order Phase.

1. **Personal locations.** Each player board has two actions you do in your own workshop, Form & Decorate and Glaze & Load, 2 spaces each, the second under a Cover token at the start. Private actions are named as verbs; public locations are the places you go to: Materials Yard, Commission Market, Guild Hall (was Guild & Academy), Canal Wharf (was Labour) and Magistrate's Hall (was Court Patronage).
2. **Decoration at forming, Glaze at loading.** Form & Decorate forms and decorates; Glaze & Load glazes and loads. There is no Glazed-but-unloaded state.
3. **Heat marker.** The Glaze & Load Shifu places the player's Heat marker face-down on one of their ceramics; after Base Heat, in First Player order, it is turned to +1 or −1 or returned.
4. **No Shifu overfill.** All capacities are absolute.
5. **No Starting Tech; up to 3 Advanced Techs, at most 1 per discipline;** 18 tiles, 6 per discipline. A Form & Decorate Tech removes the Cover token from the second Form & Decorate space, a Glazing & Loading Tech the one from the second Glaze & Load space; Firing Techs remove nothing.
6. **Orders.** Only held Orders may be completed, in Work-Phase turn order. A reserved face-up Order is replaced only after the reserving player's turn ends. The Shifu may use a top-3 selection for one reservation; Colour Samples is now a hand-limit tile.
7. **Imperial Priority** glazes and loads a Shaped vessel. **The Imperial Kiln** is fired by the court: 2 + Fire + a modifier chosen at loading, immune to Contributions.
8. **Magistrate's Hall (was Court Patronage)** is a capped location with 1 / 1 / 2 spaces (2p / 3p / 4p), usable from Recognition 0 to 2; the Shifu pays 3. The Imperial Grant is 1 Clay + 1 Wood + 1 Coin (the 3-Coin option is gone), so buying step 1 is a real cost rather than a refund.
9. **Shared Kiln** is 2 spaces per player: 4 (2-1-1), 6 (2-2-2), 8 (3-3-2).
10. **Ge** is rebuilt: the Crackle Decoration is replaced by Painted, and Ge instead crackles 1 Standard ceramic a round after Quality (it becomes Fine and keeps a Crackle marker that counts as any one Decoration for Orders).
11. **Test Pieces** resolves after Base Heat, before Heat markers (Appendix B).
12. **Fire deck:** the discard pile is open information; the deck is reshuffled only when it runs out.
13. **Order values:** O16 9 VP, O21 9 VP, O31 12 VP, O32 15 VP, O33 14 VP, O40 15 VP.
14. **Decorations all cost 2 Coins** (Plain was 1). The Shifu's Form & Decorate bonus is now **one Decoration −1 Coin when forming 2** (was total Clay −1). Decorator's Kit reduces by 1 instead of to 0. Ding's extra vessel costs 1 Clay + 1 Coin. S10 is 4 VP.
15. **Kiln Die.** At the start of every round, roll a die with faces 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3; the result is that round's starting Base Heat (was always 2). Contributions, the clamp, the Fire card and everything after are unchanged. The court-fired Imperial Kiln uses the same result in place of 2.
16. **Contribution participation** requires a ceramic in the Shared Kiln. Since the Imperial Kiln ignores Contributions, its owner no longer gets a vote on the shared fire through it alone (closes a free-sabotage hole opened by change 7).
17. **Order buffer for held-only completion.** Each player starts with 2 Starting Orders + 1 Main Order (was 1 + 1), and the hand limit is 4 (5 with Colour Samples; was 3 / 4). Reservation counts stay at Apprentice 1 / Shifu 2.
18. **Track stays optional.** No worker on the Imperial track. The Magistrate's Hall (formerly Court Patronage) is open from Recognition 0 (the Recognition-1 gate is removed) and the Grant is 1 Clay + 1 Wood + 1 Coin, which together fix the step-1 refund with fewer words and let a Coin-rich player buy into the track as a sink. Imperial Priority stays as drafted; O17–O20 stay as printed.
19. **Painted replaces Crackle** as the fourth Decoration (2 Coins). Crackle is now something only the Ge Kiln produces, in the kiln, with a marker. Cards renamed: S13, O13, O17, O23, O39, O40, O46; Decorator's Kit reads Carved, Impressed or Painted.
20. **Tech VP scored on purchase.** The 1 VP per Advanced Tech is recorded the moment the tile is taken, not at game end. Bookkeeping only; Techs cannot be lost.
21. **v1.6.2 — Commission Market and public Shifu effects.** The Market gives no resource. An Apprentice looks at the top 2 cards of the deck and reserves 1 Order from the display or those 2; the Shifu looks at the top 3 and reserves up to 2 from the display and those 3; revealed cards not reserved are discarded (the blind top-card option is gone). Materials Yard Shifu gains 6 resources (the 1-Coin option is gone). Guild Hall Shifu takes up to 2 Techs from different disciplines at printed cost (no discount), and Tech displays refill after the taking player's turn, like the Order display. Canal Wharf Shifu gains 4 Coins and takes the top Main Order card unseen.
22. **v1.6.2 — Tech re-pass.** Colour Samples adds a once-per-round swap (discard 1 held Order, look at the top 3, reserve 1). Canal Barge now gives 2 Coins once per round when you complete an Order; its Work-Phase completion gained nothing over the Order Phase. Protective Saggars: your ceramics are never Flawed and once a round 1 Standard becomes Fine; cost 3. Fuel Ledger: Bank and Stoke cost no Wood; cost 2. Second Firing reveals 2 cards and uses 1. Senior Apprentice and Kiln Tending cost 3; Test Pieces stays at 3, since a buried card changes the firing for every player, not only its owner.
23. **v1.6.2 — Starting Orders: 16 → 8, values equalised.** Four Glaze cards (White, Celadon, Grey-Green 3 VP + 4 Coins; Moon White 4 + 4), small-shape choice 3 + 4, Vase or Censer 3 + 5, Carved/Impressed/Painted 3 + 3, any 2 + 4. The old deck paid 2 VP for a Shape and 4 VP for a Decoration although both are free choices once every Decoration costs 2, so the deal could swing the start by 4–5 VP. Setup is unchanged: 2 Starting Orders + 1 Main Order each.
24. **v1.6.2 — Main Order re-pass and deck flow.** Decoration requirements no longer earn a premium (every Decoration costs 2): O05 6 + 3, O19 and O20 6 VP, O38 12, O39 13. Shape-only singles rise to 4 VP (O01–O03); the choice singles ease to O14 5 + 3 and O15 5 + 4; O04 7 + 3; O22 9. Fine doubles gain the coordination premium they lacked: O26 11, O27 12, O28 11, O30 13; the Masterpiece doubles O32 16, O33 and O34 15 + 5. Cards looked at and not reserved (Market, Colour Samples) go to the bottom of the Main Order deck instead of the discard pile, so the deck lasts a 4-player game without a reshuffle.
25. **Keyword: Select N.** Look at the top N cards of the Main Order deck, reserve 1 of them or 1 face-up Order, put the rest on the bottom. Commission Market: Apprentice Select 2, Shifu Select 3 reserving up to 2; Colour Samples: Select 3 when acquired and for its once-a-round swap. Wording only.
26. **Fuel Ledger as cards.** Wood is open information, so a Wood token tucked under a face-down Contribution card could never be secret. The hidden commitment is replaced by 2 Contribution cards that come with the tile: **Fuel Ledger Bank** (1 Wood, −2) and **Fuel Ledger Stoke** (1 Wood, +2). Its owner chooses among 5 cards and plays 1; plain Bank and Stoke stay free. Same options and prices as before. Contribution cards rise to 14 and all share one back, so a face-down card reveals nothing.
27. **Tech VP removed.** Advanced Techs no longer score 1 VP when gained. After the re-pass every tile pays for itself on ability alone (first tile: the Cover token; second: a Firing ability ≈ 4.7 VP), so the VP was only subsidising third tiles and round-4 purchases. Techs are workshop growth; Orders and Crowns are the points. Connoisseur's Cabinet keeps its end-game VP. Printed costs unchanged; watch Techs per player and Guild placements in rounds 4–5.

## Game Overview

In **Kiln Opening**, each player directs a ceramic workshop inspired by one of five renowned kiln traditions associated with the Song Dynasty. Your Shifu and Apprentices gather materials, form and decorate vessels, glaze them as they enter the kiln, compete for Orders and specialised knowledge, and share a kiln whose final firing is influenced by every participating workshop.

**Worker placement determines what your workshop prepares. The shared firing determines what that preparation becomes.** All ceramics in the firing share one Base Heat and one Fire card, so what you make depends partly on what everyone else decides to burn.

The game lasts exactly **5 rounds**.

## Setup

1. Place the Central Action Board and Shared Kiln in the centre. For the Shared Kiln: **4 players** use all 8 spaces (3 High, 3 Middle, 2 Low); **3 players** use 6 spaces, covering 1 High and 1 Middle space (2-2-2); **2 players** use 4 spaces, covering 1 High, 2 Middle and 1 Low space (2-1-1). In 2- and 3-player games also cover the second Magistrate's Hall space.
2. Shuffle the **8-card Starting Order deck** and the **48-card Main Order deck** separately. Deal each player **2 Starting Orders and 1 Main Order**. These 3 Orders form that player's starting hand and are **kept secret**.
3. Return any undealt Starting Orders to the box. Reshuffle the remaining Main Order deck, then reveal **6 face-up Main Orders** from left to right.
4. Separate the 18 Advanced Techs into Form & Decorate, Glazing & Loading and Firing decks of 6. Shuffle each discipline and reveal **2 face-up tiles from each**. Keep the 2 Fuel Ledger Contribution cards beside the Firing deck; they go to whoever acquires Fuel Ledger.
5. Shuffle the 12-card Fire deck and place it face down beside a face-up discard pile. Place the Kiln Die beside the Base Heat track.
6. Place Clay, Wood, Coins and Vessel cards within reach. Sort Vessel cards by Shape.
7. Place the **4 Imperial Kiln tiles** beside the Imperial Recognition track.
8. Each player chooses a player colour and takes 1 Shifu, 3 Apprentices, 1 Heat marker, 1 Imperial Recognition marker, 2 Cover tokens, and 1 each of Bank the Fire, Tend the Fire and Stoke the Fire. Place that colour's **Imperial Priority token** on Recognition space **3**.
9. Randomly choose the First Player. In reverse turn order, each player chooses an available Kiln Player Board and places a Cover token on the second space of its Form & Decorate and of its Glaze & Load.
10. Each player places **1 Shifu + 3 Apprentices** as available workers.
11. Leave each player's Imperial Kiln area empty until it is gained through Imperial Recognition.
12. Each player receives **2 Clay, 2 Wood and 3 Coins**.
13. Place all Imperial Recognition markers on space **0** and the Round marker on Round **1**.

## Round Structure

| Phase | Name | Summary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Start of Round | Roll the Kiln Die for this round's starting Base Heat; rotate Orders; refill displays; ready abilities. |
| 2 | Work Phase | Alternate worker placements at central and personal locations. |
| 3 | Firing Phase | Contributions → Base Heat → Test Pieces → Heat markers → Fire → Actual Heat → Quality. |
| 4 | Order Phase | Complete held Orders, one at a time, in turn order. |
| 5 | Cleanup | Return workers and markers; trim Order hands; pass First Player. |

## Phase 1 — Start of Round

Every round, first **roll the Kiln Die** and place the Base Heat marker on the result (1, 2 or 3). Base Heat starts at that value this round; the Contributions will move it from there. Then, in Rounds 2–5:

1. Discard the **2 leftmost face-up Main Orders** to the Main Order discard pile.
2. Slide the remaining **4 Main Orders** left.
3. Reveal **2 new Main Orders**, filling the two empty positions from left to right, restoring the display to **6**.

Then ready all abilities marked **once per round**, and the First Player begins the Work Phase.

## Phase 2 — Work Phase

Beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise, each player must place **1 available worker** at a legal space and resolve its action immediately. Continue until **every player has placed all of their workers**. Then proceed to the Firing Phase.

Each player has **1 Shifu and 3 Apprentices** available each round. Each worker is placed once per round, giving each player **20 worker placements over the game, including 5 Shifu placements**.

- A worker may go on any **unoccupied** space of a location. Workers already on other spaces of that location, yours or anyone's, do not matter. **Capacities are absolute:** no worker, including the Shifu, may be placed at a location whose spaces are all occupied.
- The Shifu follows the same placement rules as an Apprentice and receives the Shifu effect of its location.
- You may place a worker only if you can perform at least **1 instance of that location's main action**. "Up to 2" means **1 or 2, never 0**. Optional bonuses may still be declined.
- **You may not pass during the Work Phase.** The Canal Wharf has no capacity limit or resource cost, so it is always a legal placement for an available worker.
- **Personal locations** are on your own board. Each personal space may be used **once per round**. A space under a Cover token cannot be used.

## Phase 3 — Firing Phase

If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, **skip the Firing Phase and do not reveal a Fire card**.

| Step | What happens | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Contributions | Participating players each secretly choose 1 of their Contribution cards, then reveal and pay Wood. Start at the Kiln Die result and clamp the final result to 0–5. | **Base Heat** |
| 2. Test Pieces | In First Player order, a Test Pieces owner may look at the top Fire card and bury it. | — |
| 3. Heat markers | In First Player order, each player with a face-down Heat marker on a ceramic turns it to +1 or −1, or returns it. Kiln Master places its marker now. | — |
| 4. Fire | Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier. | **Global Heat** |
| 5. Position | Shared-Kiln ceramics: Global Heat + zone modifier (High +1 / Middle 0 / Low −1, or the Kiln Furniture modifier) + Heat marker. Imperial Kiln ceramics: Kiln Die result + Fire modifier + the modifier chosen at loading + Heat marker. | **Actual Heat** |
| 6. Compare | Compare Actual Heat with Preferred Heat after applicable abilities. | **Quality** |

- **Base Heat and Global Heat belong to the whole firing. Actual Heat belongs to one ceramic.**
- Clamp Base Heat to 0–5. Global Heat and Actual Heat are not clamped.

### Contribution Cards

Every player has the same 3 cards:

| Contribution | Wood | Heat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank the Fire | 1 | −1 |
| Tend the Fire | 0 | 0 |
| Stoke the Fire | 1 | +1 |

A player participates in Contributions **only if they have at least 1 ceramic in the Shared Kiln. A ceramic in an Imperial Kiln is fired by the court and gives no say over the shared fire**. Tend is always affordable. The owner of **Fuel Ledger** holds 2 more cards, **Fuel Ledger Bank** (1 Wood, −2) and **Fuel Ledger Stoke** (1 Wood, +2), and pays no Wood for plain Bank or Stoke; they still play exactly 1 card. After the firing, return all Contribution cards to their owners.

**Base Heat = the Kiln Die result + the sum of final Contribution modifiers, clamped to 0–5.** Examples with the die showing 2: Tend + Tend + Tend → 2. Stoke + Tend + Tend → 3. Bank + Tend + Tend → 1. Stoke + Bank + Tend → 2; both players still pay their Wood. With the die showing 3: Tend + Tend + Tend → 3; Bank + Tend + Tend → 2; Stoke + Stoke + Tend → 5.

### Heat Markers

- Each player owns 1 Heat marker, with a +1 face and a −1 face. The Glaze & Load Shifu places it **face-down** on 1 of that player's ceramics in the Shared Kiln or Imperial Kiln.
- After Base Heat is determined, in First Player order, each player with a face-down marker **turns it to +1 or −1, or returns it to their supply**.
- A Heat marker adds its value to that ceramic's Actual Heat. It is not a zone modifier: it stacks with Kiln Furniture, and it stays in place for a Second Firing recalculation.
- A ceramic may carry only 1 Heat marker. Return markers when the ceramic leaves the kiln.

### Imperial Kiln

When you first reach **Imperial Recognition 2**, take an Imperial Kiln tile and place it in your Imperial Kiln area.

- The Imperial Kiln holds **1 ceramic per firing**.
- During a Glaze & Load action, 1 vessel loaded by that action may be placed in your empty Imperial Kiln instead of the Shared Kiln. Imperial Priority also loads it. No Tech effect loads it.
- Its ceramic **is fired by the court's own kilnmen. When you load it, choose a modifier of −1, 0 or +1 and note it. Its Actual Heat is the Kiln Die result + the Fire modifier + that modifier, plus any Heat marker, whatever the Contributions were. It still fires during the shared Firing Phase**.
- It counts as a ceramic in this firing for your Techs and abilities, but it does not make you a Contribution participant: only a ceramic in the Shared Kiln does. It does not occupy a Shared Kiln space.
- After firing, its ceramic resolves Quality and moves to the owner's Finished Ceramics area normally.

At **Imperial Recognition 3**, take your coloured Imperial Priority token from the track. **Once per game, before or after your worker action, spend this token to give 1 of your Shaped vessels a Glaze and load it into your empty Imperial Kiln.** It is a token effect, not a Glaze & Load action.

## Phase 4 — Order Phase

Beginning with the **First Player** and proceeding clockwise, each player may complete **any number of held Orders, one at a time**. Only Orders in your hand can be completed. **Face-up Main Orders cannot be completed**; they are reserved at the Commission Market during the Work Phase. The display does not change during the Order Phase.

See **Completing Orders** below for the procedure.

## Phase 5 — Cleanup

1. Return all workers to their owners. Return any Setter's Mark tile still on the kiln.
2. Discard held Orders of your choice down to your hand limit of **4** (5 with Colour Samples). Return discarded Starting Orders to the box; place discarded Main Orders in the Main Order discard pile.
3. If this was Round 5, proceed to final scoring. Otherwise, pass the First Player marker clockwise and advance the Round marker.

## Ceramic Production

### Production Stages

1. Gain Clay and Wood (Materials Yard).
2. Form a vessel and choose its Decoration (Form & Decorate).
3. Give it a Glaze and load it into the Shared Kiln or, if available, your Imperial Kiln (Glaze & Load).
4. Resolve the common Firing Phase.
5. Use the finished ceramic to complete a held Order or keep it for the end-game Exhibition.

Advanced Techs may compress these stages.

## Workshop Development

A player's long-term workshop engine consists of:

> **1 Kiln Tradition + up to 3 Advanced Techs, 1 per discipline + up to 2 second personal spaces, their Cover tokens removed**

### Tech Effects and Worker Actions

**A Tech effect is not a worker action.** If an Advanced Tech lets you perform part of a worker action, such as forming, glazing or loading, that does **not** count as taking that worker action and does not trigger effects that require that action, unless explicitly stated otherwise. Imperial Priority is likewise a token effect.

### Advanced Tech Rules

- Exactly 6 Form & Decorate, 6 Glazing & Loading and 6 Firing Advanced Techs exist.
- A player may own at most 3, and at most 1 from each discipline.
- A Form & Decorate Tech removes the Cover token from your second Form & Decorate space; a Glazing & Loading Tech removes the one from your second Glaze & Load space; Firing Techs remove nothing.
- Follow each tile's printed timing and usage limit.
- If you control multiple abilities with the same timing, choose the order in which you resolve them. If several players have abilities in the same timing window, resolve players in First Player order; each player chooses the order of their own abilities.

## Orders and Imperial Recognition

### Main Order Market

There is one **48-card Main Order deck** and one **6-card public display**. The separate 8-card Starting Order deck is used only during Setup.

The display is ordered from oldest to newest: the **leftmost Order is the oldest** and the **rightmost Order is the newest**.

A face-up Main Order leaves the display only when it is **reserved** at the Commission Market or **discarded at the start of a round**. When one is reserved, leave its position empty until the reserving player's turn ends; then slide every Order to its right one position left and reveal Main Orders into the rightmost empty positions, restoring the display to 6. If a single turn takes more than one face-up Order, refill after the turn, not between the takes.

Orders may award **VP, Coins and 0–3 Crowns**. Commercial Orders tend to be more flexible and pay more Coins. Crown Orders tend to be more specific, pay fewer Coins and advance Imperial Recognition.

### Reserving Orders

Use the Commission Market during the Work Phase to reserve Main Orders: an Apprentice Selects 2, the Shifu Selects 3 and reserves up to 2. The Canal Wharf Shifu takes the top card of the deck unseen. Acquiring **Colour Samples** lets you Select 3 at once without a Commission Market action, raises your hand limit to 5 and lets you swap 1 held Order for a Select 3 once a round; see Appendix B.

A held Order:

- is in your **secret hand** and is protected from other players;
- counts toward your hand limit during Cleanup;
- can be completed during any later Order Phase;
- scores exactly as printed.

### Completing Orders

On your turn in the Order Phase, for each Order you complete:

1. Choose 1 held Order and **reveal it**.
2. Choose the required finished, undelivered ceramics.
3. Confirm all Shape, Glaze, Decoration, relationship and minimum Quality requirements.
4. Place the used ceramics beneath the completed Order.
5. Record the printed VP.
6. Gain the printed Coins.
7. Resolve **1 Crown at a time**. Each Crown advances Imperial Recognition by 1 until you reach Recognition 4. After you are at Recognition 4, each further Crown scores **1 VP immediately**. Resolve every milestone crossed.
8. Place the completed Order face up in your scoring area.

A ceramic may satisfy only one completed Order.

#### Reading Multi-Ceramic Requirements

Requirements are **independent unless a specific pairing is explicitly printed**. If an Order says **"Bowl + Brush Washer; one White and one Moon White; one Plain and one Impressed"**, you must use exactly one Bowl and one Brush Washer, exactly one White and one Moon White Glaze, and exactly one Plain and one Impressed Decoration, assigned between the two Shapes however you like. If a card intends a fixed pairing, it writes the pairing directly, for example **"Bowl: White + Plain; Brush Washer: Moon White + Impressed."**

### Imperial Recognition Track

Imperial Recognition may be advanced by **Crown icons on completed Orders** or by **paying at the Magistrate's Hall**. The Magistrate's Hall cannot advance you to Recognition 4.

- If several players resolve abilities in the same window, resolve them beginning with the First Player and proceeding clockwise.
- Jun acts after Actual Heat is calculated (Heat markers included) and before Quality is assigned.
- Ge's Crackle is an after-Quality effect; Ge chooses its order among their own after-Quality effects.

## End of the Game and Scoring

The game ends after Round 5 Cleanup. Score:

1. all VP recorded during play: Orders, Kiln Tradition abilities, **Imperial Audience** and VP from Crowns gained beyond Recognition 4;
2. the end-game Exhibition;
3. **1 VP per 3 Coins remaining**, maximum **5 VP**.

The player with the most VP wins.

**Tie Breakers:** 1. Farther along Imperial Recognition. 2. More Crown icons printed across completed Orders. 3. More Masterpiece ceramics delivered or exhibited. 4. If still tied, share victory.

## General Clarifications

- **Unlimited resources:** The supply of Clay, Wood and Coins is unlimited. If the supplied components run out, use suitable substitutes, such as spare tokens or counters, or record the additional amounts on paper. Make each substitute's resource type and value clear to all players.
- Gain Clay, Wood and Coins from the general supply and return all spent Clay, Wood and Coins to it.
- **Resources are open information.** Each player's total Clay, Wood and Coins must be clear to all players and accurately stated on request. Contribution choices remain secret until revealed; Wood is paid only on reveal, so no Wood is set aside beforehand.
- **No trading:** Players may not trade Clay, Wood, Coins, Orders, Techs or ceramics.
- **Negotiation:** Players may discuss and make promises about their intended Contributions and Heat markers. These promises are non-binding. Players must accurately disclose public information and may not transfer resources or other game components between players.
- **Orders in hand are secret information.** The number of Orders in each player's hand is public.
- **All undelivered ceramics are open information**, including Shaped, Loaded and Finished ceramics and all recorded attributes on them.
- Shaped, Loaded and Finished ceramics remain between rounds and keep all recorded attributes until a rule explicitly changes, delivers or discards them.
- Uncompleted Orders and unused ceramics have no end-game penalty.
- If the Main Order deck is empty when a card is needed, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
- **The Fire discard pile is open information.** The Fire deck is not reshuffled during the game; only if it is empty when a card is needed, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.
- If an Advanced Tech discipline cannot refill its display to 2, leave the display partially empty.

## Appendix D — Full Firing Sequence

If no ceramics are loaded in the Shared Kiln or any Imperial Kiln, **skip this phase and do not reveal a Fire card**.

1. **Choose Contributions.** Every player with at least 1 ceramic in the Shared Kiln secretly chooses 1 of their Contribution cards (Bank, Tend or Stoke; Fuel Ledger's owner also has Fuel Ledger Bank and Fuel Ledger Stoke) and must be able to pay its Wood cost.
2. **Reveal and pay.** Reveal all Contribution cards simultaneously, then pay all costs.
3. **Determine Base Heat.** Start at the Kiln Die result, add all final Contribution modifiers, then clamp to 0–5.
4. **Test Pieces.** In First Player order, each Test Pieces owner with a ceramic in this firing may pay 1 Wood to look at the top Fire card and may bury it.
5. **Heat markers.** In First Player order, each player with a face-down Heat marker on a ceramic turns it to **+1 or −1**, or returns it to their supply. Kiln Master owners place their marker in this step.
6. **Reveal Fire.** Reveal the top Fire card and add its modifier to Base Heat. This is Global Heat and is not capped.
7. **Calculate Actual Heat.** Shared-Kiln ceramics: Global Heat + zone modifier (or the Kiln Furniture modifier) + Heat marker. Imperial Kiln ceramics: Kiln Die result + Fire modifier + the modifier chosen at loading + Heat marker.
8. **Heat-adjustment abilities.** In First Player order, resolve Jun and any other abilities that occur after Actual Heat is calculated and before Quality is assigned.
9. **Assign Quality.** Compare final Actual Heat with Preferred Heat.
10. **After-Quality abilities.** In First Player order, resolve Protective Saggars, Second Firing, Ge's Crackle and similar effects. If you control multiple abilities in this window, choose their order.
11. **Salvage a Flawed ceramic, if desired.** Each player may discard 1 ceramic still Flawed from this firing to gain 2 Coins. Return its Vessel card to the matching Shape supply.
12. **Finish firing.** Move all remaining fired ceramics to their owners' Finished Ceramics areas. Empty all Shared Kiln and Imperial Kiln spaces. Return Heat markers, the Kiln Master marker and Kiln Furniture tiles to their owners, discard the Fire card face up, and return all Contribution cards.

## Appendix E — Quick Reference

### Round Sequence

1. **Start:** roll the Kiln Die and set the starting Base Heat (1, 2 or 3); Rounds 2–5 discard the 2 leftmost Main Orders, slide the remaining 4 left and reveal 2 new Orders to restore the **6-card display**; ready abilities.
2. **Work:** First Player clockwise; each player must place 1 worker per turn. No passing; end when every player has placed all their workers.
3. **Fire:** Contributions → Base Heat → Test Pieces → Heat markers → Fire → Actual Heat → abilities → Quality.
4. **Orders:** First Player clockwise; each player completes any number of held Orders, one at a time. Face-up Orders cannot be completed.
5. **Cleanup:** return workers and markers; trim Orders to your hand limit (4; 5 with Colour Samples); if the game continues, pass First Player and advance the round.

### Action Summary

**Capacity:** Materials Yard, Commission Market and Guild Hall use 2 / 3 / 4 spaces in 2 / 3 / 4-player games. The Canal Wharf is uncapped. The Magistrate's Hall has 1 / 1 / 2 spaces, usable at Recognition 0–2. Form & Decorate and Glaze & Load are on your board: 2 spaces each, the second under a Cover token until the matching Tech removes it. Every capacity is absolute.

### Imperial Recognition

- **1 Crown — Imperial Grant:** gain **1 Clay, 1 Wood and 1 Coin**.
- **2 Crowns — Imperial Gift:** gain your **Imperial Kiln tile** (court-fired: Kiln Die result + Fire + chosen modifier, immune to Contributions).
- **3 Crowns — Imperial Priority:** take your coloured token. Once per game, before or after your worker action, spend it to glaze and load **1 Shaped vessel** into your empty Imperial Kiln.
- **4 Crowns — Imperial Audience:** gain **6 VP**. After Recognition 4, each additional Crown scores **1 VP immediately**.
- Crowns on completed Orders and the Magistrate's Hall advance Recognition; the Magistrate's Hall cannot reach Recognition 4.

### Tech Rule

A Tech effect is **not a worker action**. It does not count toward location visits or trigger effects requiring that worker action unless explicitly stated. A worker placement must perform at least **1 instance of the location's main action**; "up to 2" means **1 or 2, never 0**.
