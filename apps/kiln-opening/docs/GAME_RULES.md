# GAME_RULES.md — V1.6.2 source index

- [V1.6.2 Player Rulebook](./KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_EN_SOURCE.md) is the only mechanical authority, designated by the owner on 2026-10-17. Its [Chinese edition](./KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_ZH.md) and [component text](./KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_COMPONENT_TEXT.md) are the owner's matching files; where they differ, the English rulebook wins.
- [V1.6.2 audit](./RULEBOOK_AUDIT_V1.6.2.md) records the source checksums, the owner's two revisions of 2026-10-17 (the Guild Hall; then changes 23–24: an 8-card Starting deck S01–S08, nineteen re-valued Main Orders, and looked-at Orders not reserved going to the bottom of the Main Order deck), owner amendments A1 and A2, owner rulings R1–R19 and B1–B3 as re-checked for V1.6.2 (R15–R18 of 2026-10-17 confirm the engine track's first defaults; R19 of 2026-10-18 sends several looked cards under the Main Order deck in random order, replacing provisional default P5; revision 2 makes R3 obsolete and supersedes R14), editorial corrections, recorded interpretations, provisional defaults P1–P3, and the open questions for the owner (none are open).
- [V1.6.2 engine delta](./ENGINE_V1.6.2_DELTA.md) lists every change V1.6.2 needs in the engine, data and tests. The [V1.6.1 engine design](./ENGINE_V1.6.1_DESIGN.md) remains the base contract wherever the delta is silent.
- [Chinese glossary](./GLOSSARY_ZH_V1.6.1.md) fixes Simplified Chinese terms. Chinese text uses a Song-dynasty kiln-workshop register rather than literal translation.
- `data/*.json` derives component definitions, costs, reminders and localized rules text from the V1.6.2 rulebook and its Chinese edition. The reminders come from the [repository copy of the component text](./KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_COMPONENT_TEXT_SOURCE.md) (the owner's file, bold removed), and the rules-details clarifications from [KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_CLARIFICATIONS.md](./KILN_OPENING_v1.6.2_CLARIFICATIONS.md), which replace the V1.6.1 files of the same kind.
- [Implementation decisions](./IMPLEMENTATION_DECISIONS.md) and the [online specification](./ONLINE_GAME_SPEC.md) document digital behaviour without changing the board-game rules.
- [V1.6.1 implementation plan](./V1.6_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) tracks the migration phases.
