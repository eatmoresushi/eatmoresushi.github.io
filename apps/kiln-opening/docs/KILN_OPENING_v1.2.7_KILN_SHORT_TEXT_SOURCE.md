# V1.2.7 Kiln hover and workshop wording — owner amendment, 2026-09-20

These owner-approved reminders replace the original component draft's short Kiln descriptions on hover and in Your workshop. Full clicked descriptions and game mechanics remain unchanged. Ge uses the shorter wording subsequently approved by the owner, with one line break between its two effects.

### Ru Kiln / 汝窑 — Quiet Perfection

Once per round, when you complete an Order using a Masterpiece with Celadon Glaze and Plain Decoration, gain 4 VP.

### Guan Kiln / 官窑 — Imperial Patronage

Once per round, when you complete a Crown Order, gain 2 Coins and 1 VP.

### Ge Kiln / 哥窑 — Crackle Mastery

For Orders and Exhibition scoring, your Standard Crackle ceramics count as Fine.
Once per round, 1 of your Crackle ceramics used for an Order may count as any one Decoration for all that Order’s requirements.

### Ding Kiln / 定窑 — Moulded Production

Once per round, during a Potter’s Wheel action, after forming a Bowl, Plate or Brush Washer, you may pay 1 Clay to form 1 extra vessel of the same Shape.

### Jun Kiln / 钧窑 — Kiln Transformation

Once per round, after Actual Heat is calculated, but before Quality is assigned, you may pay 1 Wood to adjust 1 of your ceramics’ Actual Heat by +1 or −1.
